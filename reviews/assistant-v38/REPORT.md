## Adjudication boundaries

Default adds unsupported controlled-environment, prior-communication and deployment-readiness claims. House removes those additions but omits an explicit strategy recommendation. Both readouts omit the required derived reduction while retaining correct endpoints. The default signatory wording and house team-activity claim remain unresolved. The house vendor memo explicitly requests signing authorization, which is a proposal rather than a claim that the signatory role is already assigned.

The 16 calls cost $0.245184 versus Gemini Flash at $0.0428445. Pro has more house drafts ready without edits (5/8 versus 3/8), but fewer default content-ready drafts (3/8 versus 5/8). This mixed small-sample result does not establish a reliable winner.
