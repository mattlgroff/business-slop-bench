Prices are the saved October 7 Gateway base rates. Observed cost covers the eight represented successful generations, including reported reasoning and cache costs where applicable. A more expensive model does not automatically rank higher.

Astra's primary house result was 8/8 ready without edits. Both full repeats returned 6/8 while preserving 54/54 content checks. The table retains the original primary attempt for every model, without selecting the best output per task.

Opus is the strongest Claude in this comparison. Sonnet and Haiku each produced two drafts ready without edits, but Sonnet passed two more content checks. Haiku's eight drafts cost about fifteen times less than Sonnet's. Fable produced three sendable drafts but passed fewer content checks than Sonnet and cost more than Astra.

Content ready and ready without edits are different: the latter also requires the word limit, no drafting residue, and no supported style finding. Unresolved content checks earn no point. All grades are provisional unblinded assistant judgments on eight synthetic briefs, without independent human gold.

Fable lacks a ZDR route in the saved evidence and is excluded from the separate eligibility-filtered leaderboard. That does not change its writing score here.
