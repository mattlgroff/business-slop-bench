# Proposal for Approval: Four-Week Discovery Engagement

## Scope and Deliverables

We propose a **four-week discovery-only engagement** producing three artifacts:

1. A current-state process map  
2. A prioritized backlog  
3. An implementation recommendation  

Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Commercial Offer

- **Effort:** 80 hours  
- **Rate:** $200 per hour  
- **Total fee:** **$16,000**  
- **Payment:** 50% ($8,000) at kickoff and 50% ($8,000) on delivery  

## Client Responsibilities

The client will supply interview access and source documents.

## How to Proceed

To approve the engagement, the sponsor should sign the proposal and provide a named client contact. The start date will be agreed only after both requirements are met.

At delivery, the sponsor will confirm delivery of the three listed artifacts as acceptance of the engagement.
