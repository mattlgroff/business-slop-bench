# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**Decision:** Select **Alpha** for year one.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| **Total year-one cost** | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Within $100,000 ceiling, including setup | Yes | Yes |
| Required SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Met | Met |

Both vendors fit the budget, but cost is not the deciding constraint. SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha satisfies that requirement; Beta does not and has no committed SSO delivery date. Beta’s $22,000 year-one savings therefore do not make it an eligible choice for production.

Alpha leaves **$8,000** beneath the year-one budget ceiling.

## Recommendation and Approval Path

Approve Alpha’s **$92,000 year-one cost** and proceed with contracting. Do not select Beta for year one because it fails the mandatory SSO constraint.

Priya should own procurement and coordinate the contract process, but must not sign. After CFO approval, **Alex, the commercial director and sole authorized contract signatory, should sign**. The CFO’s approval does not delegate signing authority to Priya or anyone else.
