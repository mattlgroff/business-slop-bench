# Decision memo: Year-one vendor selection

**To:** CFO  
**Recommendation:** Select Alpha and approve its $92,000 year-one cost.

Alpha combines a $72,000 annual subscription with $20,000 setup. The total is $8,000 below the $100,000 budget ceiling. Required SSO is available, and Alpha meets the other stated requirements. Production must still wait until SSO is in place.

Beta costs $70,000 in year one, comprising a $60,000 subscription and $10,000 setup. It is $22,000 cheaper than Alpha and meets the other stated requirements. Its required SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date. Since SSO is mandatory before production, Beta cannot enter production unless that capability becomes available. Its lower price does not resolve the production blocker or provide a dated path to resolving it.

For year one, Alpha is the only vendor that satisfies the budget ceiling and has the required SSO available. Reject Beta for this purchase.

Priya owns procurement and should coordinate the contract process. The CFO must approve the Alpha contract before signature. Alex, the commercial director and sole authorized signatory, must then sign it. The CFO’s approval does not delegate signing authority to Priya or anyone else.
