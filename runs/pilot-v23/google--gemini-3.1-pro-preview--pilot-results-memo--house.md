**Recommendation**
Do not scale the pilot. 

**Results**
* Handling time dropped from 15 minutes at the 200-ticket baseline to 12 minutes in the 100-ticket pilot.
* The quality pass rate fell from 94% at baseline to 89%, missing the 92% required scaling threshold.
* The pilot lacked randomized allocation and evaluated a different ticket mix than the baseline.
* No staffing reductions or cash savings were measured.
