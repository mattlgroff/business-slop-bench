# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**Subject:** Approval to select Alpha for year one

## Recommendation

Approve **Alpha** for year one and authorize Priya, procurement owner, to proceed toward signing.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Within $100,000 ceiling | Yes | Yes |
| Required SSO available | Yes | No |
| Eligible for production | Yes | No |

Both vendors meet the other stated requirements and fall within the year-one budget ceiling, including setup. Alpha leaves **$8,000** under the ceiling. Beta is **$22,000** less expensive than Alpha but cannot satisfy the mandatory requirement that SSO be available before production. Beta also has no committed SSO delivery date.

## Decision Rationale

Alpha is the only vendor that meets all stated constraints in year one. Beta’s lower cost does not offset its failure to provide mandatory SSO; selecting it would prevent production under the stated requirements.

## Approval Requested

CFO approval to select **Alpha at a year-one cost of $92,000** before Priya proceeds with signing.
