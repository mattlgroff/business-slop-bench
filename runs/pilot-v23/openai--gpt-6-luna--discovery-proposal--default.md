# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Purpose and scope
A four-week discovery engagement to establish the current state and recommend next steps. Deliverables:

- Current-state process map
- Prioritized backlog
- Implementation recommendation

## Commercial offer
**Fixed total fee: $16,000**, based on 80 hours at $200 per hour. Payment is split equally: **$8,000 at kickoff** and **$8,000 on delivery**.

Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Client participation
The client supplies access to interview participants and relevant source documents.

## Acceptance and start
The sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts. The start date will be agreed only after signature and designation of a named client contact.

## How to proceed
To proceed, sign the proposal and name a client contact. The start date can then be agreed.
