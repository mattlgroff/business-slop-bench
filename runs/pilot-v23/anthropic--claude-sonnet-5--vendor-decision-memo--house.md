# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO
**From:** Procurement (Priya)
**Re:** Vendor selection, Alpha vs. Beta

## Facts

- Budget ceiling: $100,000 including setup.
- Alpha: $72,000 subscription plus $20,000 setup, total $92,000. SSO available.
- Beta: $60,000 subscription plus $10,000 setup, total $70,000. SSO unavailable, no delivery date.
- SSO is mandatory before production for either vendor.

## Analysis

Beta costs $22,000 less than Alpha in year one. That saving is not usable because Beta cannot meet the SSO requirement and has committed no date to fix it. Signing Beta now would leave the company unable to reach production until SSO ships, on a schedule nobody can confirm.

Alpha meets every stated requirement, including SSO, at $92,000, which is under the $100,000 ceiling with $8,000 of headroom.

## Recommendation

Select Alpha for year one. It is the only vendor that satisfies the mandatory SSO requirement and fits within budget.

## Next Steps

- Priya to issue the Alpha contract for CFO signature.
- CFO to approve the $92,000 year-one spend.
- Revisit Beta in a future cycle only if it commits to a firm SSO delivery date.
