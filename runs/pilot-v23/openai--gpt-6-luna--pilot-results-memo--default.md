## Executive readout: Do not scale the pilot now

**What the results support:** Mean handling time was 12 minutes in the pilot versus 15 minutes at baseline, a 3-minute difference. This is a directional efficiency signal, not evidence that the pilot caused the improvement: ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized.

**Why not scale:** Quality pass rate fell from 94% at baseline to 89% in the pilot, below the required 92% scaling threshold. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

**Next decision:** The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. Use that follow-up to assess handling time and quality on comparable tickets before reconsidering scale.
