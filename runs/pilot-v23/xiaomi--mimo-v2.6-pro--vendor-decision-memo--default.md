# DECISION MEMO: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO
**From:** Procurement (Priya, owner)
**Subject:** Vendor selection — recommendation for CFO approval before signing

## Recommendation

**Select Alpha.** It is the only vendor that satisfies the mandatory SSO constraint and stays within the year-one budget ceiling.

## Year-One Cost

| Vendor | Subscription | Setup | Year-One Total | Within $100,000 Ceiling? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha | $72,000 | $20,000 | $92,000 | Yes |
| Beta | $60,000 | $10,000 | $70,000 | Yes |

Both vendors fit the $100,000 budget ceiling including setup.

## Constraint Evaluation

**SSO is mandatory before production.** This is a gating requirement, not a preference.

- **Alpha:** Required SSO is available. Constraint met.
- **Beta:** Required SSO is unavailable, and there is no committed delivery date. Constraint not met.

Both vendors meet all other stated requirements, so SSO is the sole differentiator on compliance.

## Analysis

Beta is $22,000 cheaper in year one, but its inability to meet a mandatory pre-production requirement makes it non-compliant regardless of price. With no committed delivery date, there is no basis to treat Beta's SSO gap as a temporary shortfall that could be mitigated within year one. Selecting Beta would mean entering production without a mandatory capability, contrary to the stated constraints. The price advantage does not, and cannot, cure this failure.

Alpha meets the mandatory SSO requirement and totals $92,000 in year one, leaving $8,000 of headroom under the $100,000 ceiling.

## Requested Action

CFO approval to sign with **Alpha** for year one. Priya will proceed with procurement execution upon approval.
