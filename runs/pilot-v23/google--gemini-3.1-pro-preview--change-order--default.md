**Change Order Proposal: CRM Connector**

**Background and Requested Work**
The existing signed scope of work covers the reporting dashboard. Recently, the client requested the addition of a CRM connector. Because this was not included in the original fee, we are submitting this change-order proposal for your review.

**Pricing**
The effort required to build the CRM connector is estimated at 40 hours at a rate of $175/hour. Therefore, we are proposing a fixed change fee of $7,000 to complete this additional work. 

**Schedule Effect and Dependencies**
Adding the connector to the project will extend the timeline by two weeks. Please note that this is an estimate, not an approved calendar date. The two-week timeframe will commence only after the following conditions are met:
1. Written approval of this change order is received.
2. The client provides the necessary sandbox credentials. 

**Approval**
As the project sponsor, you must approve this change in writing before any work on the CRM connector begins. 

Please review the proposed scope, fee, and schedule impact above. Reply to this document with your written approval to proceed, or let us know if you choose to decline this additional work.
