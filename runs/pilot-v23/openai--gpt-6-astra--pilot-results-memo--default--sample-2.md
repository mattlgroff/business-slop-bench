## Executive Readout: Do Not Scale Yet

**Recommendation:** Do not scale the pilot now. The pilot’s quality pass rate of **89%** is below the required **92%** scaling threshold.

**What the results support**
- **Lower observed handling time:** Mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets versus 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets—a 3-minute, or 20%, reduction.
- **Not a demonstrated causal improvement:** Ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the comparison does not establish that the pilot caused the reduction.
- **A quality concern:** Quality pass rate was 89% in the pilot versus 94% at baseline, a decline of 5 percentage points. The pilot missed the scaling threshold by 3 percentage points.
- **No demonstrated cash savings:** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured. Lower handling time should not be presented as realized financial savings.

**Next decision:** The COO should authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up rather than approve scaling. The follow-up should test whether lower handling time persists with comparable tickets while meeting the quality threshold of at least 92%.

Revisit scaling after those results are available; the current evidence supports further evaluation, not rollout.
