## Executive readout: Do not scale yet

The pilot’s mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets. This supports further testing of a possible handling-time improvement, **not a conclusion that the pilot caused it**: ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized.

Quality pass rate fell from 94% at baseline to 89% in the pilot, below the 92% threshold required for scaling. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

**Recommendation:** Do not authorize scaling now. The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam’s matched-ticket follow-up. Use that follow-up to assess whether the handling-time difference holds with comparable tickets and whether quality meets the scaling threshold.
