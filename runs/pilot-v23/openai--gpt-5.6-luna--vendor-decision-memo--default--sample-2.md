# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**Subject:** Recommendation between Alpha and Beta

## Recommendation

Approve **Alpha** for year one and authorize Priya to proceed with procurement, subject to CFO approval before signing.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Within $100,000 budget | Yes | Yes |
| Required SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Production eligibility | Meets mandatory SSO constraint | Does not meet mandatory SSO constraint |

Both vendors meet the other stated requirements. However, SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha has SSO available, while Beta does not and has no committed delivery date. Therefore, Beta cannot satisfy the production constraint based on the available information, despite its $22,000 lower year-one cost.

Alpha’s year-one cost of $92,000 is within the $100,000 budget ceiling, including setup, leaving $8,000 of budget capacity.

## Decision

Select **Alpha**. The additional cost is necessary to meet the mandatory SSO requirement and enable production. Priya owns procurement, and the CFO’s approval is required before signing.
