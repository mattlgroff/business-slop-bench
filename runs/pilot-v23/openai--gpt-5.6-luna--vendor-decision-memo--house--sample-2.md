# Decision memo: Year-one vendor selection

**To:** CFO  
**Decision requested:** Approve Alpha for year one and authorize Priya to proceed with procurement.

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one cost | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Within $100,000 budget | Yes | Yes |
| Required SSO | Available | Unavailable, with no committed delivery date |
| Production eligibility | Meets the mandatory SSO requirement | Does not meet the mandatory SSO requirement |

Alpha costs $22,000 more in year one, leaving $8,000 under the budget ceiling. It satisfies SSO and the other stated requirements.

Beta costs less and meets the other stated requirements, but it cannot meet the mandatory production condition because SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date. Selecting Beta would therefore prevent production use until SSO becomes available.

**Recommendation:** Choose Alpha. Approve the $92,000 year-one spend and authorize Priya to sign after CFO approval.
