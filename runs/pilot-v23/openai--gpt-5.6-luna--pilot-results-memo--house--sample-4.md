# Pilot executive readout

## Recommendation

Do not scale the pilot now. Authorize a matched-ticket follow-up before making a scale decision.

## What the results support

| Measure | Baseline | Pilot | Readout |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Tickets | 200 | 100 | Smaller pilot sample |
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | 3-minute reduction observed |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | Below the 92% scaling threshold |
| Staffing or cash savings | Not measured | Not measured | No savings case established |

The pilot shows lower mean handling time, but ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized. The observed reduction therefore does not establish that the pilot caused the improvement. Quality fell to 89%, below the required 92% threshold. The pilot also produced no measured staffing reductions or cash savings.

## Next decision

The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run the matched-ticket follow-up. That study should test handling time and quality on comparable tickets before any scale authorization. Scale remains unsupported until quality reaches at least 92% and the performance result is tested with matched tickets.
