# Executive Readout: Pilot Scale Decision

**Recommendation: Do not scale the pilot now. Authorize the matched-ticket follow-up.**

## Results

| Measure | Baseline | Pilot | Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volume | 200 tickets | 100 tickets | — |
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | — |

## What the results support

- **Speed:** The pilot showed a lower mean handling time (12 vs. 15 minutes). However, the ticket mix differed from baseline and allocation was not randomized, so this difference cannot be attributed to the pilot intervention with confidence.
- **Quality:** The pilot does not support scaling. The 89% quality pass rate falls below the required 92% threshold, and is lower than the 94% baseline.
- **Cost:** No conclusion is available. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

## What the results do not support

- The pilot does not demonstrate a quality-safe, causally credible handling-time gain.
- The pilot does not demonstrate any measured financial benefit.
- These gaps are the reason to hold scaling rather than proceed: scaling now would embed an unverified process change that currently fails a mandatory quality gate.
