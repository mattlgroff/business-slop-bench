# Proposal: Four-Week Discovery Engagement

## Approval Requested
Approve a four-week, discovery-only engagement for a total fee of **$16,000**.

## Scope and Deliverables
The engagement will deliver three artifacts:
- Current-state process map
- Prioritized backlog
- Implementation recommendation

Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Commercial Offer
The fee is based on **80 hours at $200 per hour**, totaling **$16,000**, payable:
- **50% ($8,000) at kickoff**
- **50% ($8,000) on delivery**

## Client Responsibilities and Acceptance
The client will supply interview access and source documents. Acceptance consists of the sponsor confirming delivery of the three listed artifacts.

## How to Proceed
To proceed, sign the proposal and name a client contact. The start date will be agreed only after signature and designation of that contact.
