## Executive readout: do not scale the pilot now

Mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets. That supports further testing of a possible time reduction, but the different ticket mix and nonrandom allocation prevent a reliable estimate of the pilot’s effect.

Quality pass rate fell from 94% at baseline to 89% in the pilot, below the 92% scaling threshold. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

**Next decision:** The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam’s matched-ticket follow-up. Reconsider scaling after that test shows whether the handling-time difference holds and quality meets the threshold.
