# Executive Readout: Do Not Scale Yet

## Recommendation
Do not scale the pilot now. Authorize a matched-ticket follow-up before making a scaling decision.

## What the Results Support
The pilot’s mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets. This indicates a potential handling-time improvement.

However, the ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the results do not establish that the pilot caused the observed reduction.

Quality also fell from a 94% baseline pass rate to 89% in the pilot. The pilot therefore missed the required scaling threshold of at least 92%.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

## Next Decision
The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. That follow-up should determine whether handling-time improvement persists under more comparable ticket conditions and whether quality meets the 92% scaling threshold. A scale decision should follow those results.
