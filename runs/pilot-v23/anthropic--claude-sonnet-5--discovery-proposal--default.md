# Discovery Engagement Proposal

**To:** Client Sponsor
**Re:** Approval to Proceed – Four-Week Discovery Engagement

## Purpose
This proposal requests sponsor approval to launch a four-week discovery engagement focused exclusively on assessing current-state operations and defining a clear path forward.

## Scope
The engagement will deliver three artifacts:
1. Current-state process map
2. Prioritized backlog
3. Implementation recommendation

This is a discovery-only engagement. Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are explicitly excluded from this scope and would be addressed in a future phase, if approved.

## Commercial Offer

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200/hour |
| **Total Fee** | **$16,000** |
| Payment Terms | 50% due at kickoff, 50% due on delivery |

## Client Responsibilities
To enable timely execution, the client agrees to:
- Provide interview access to relevant staff and stakeholders
- Supply source documents required for process mapping and analysis

## Timeline
The start date will be confirmed only after:
- Signed agreement is received, and
- A named client contact is designated for coordination

## Acceptance Criteria
The engagement will be considered complete upon sponsor confirmation of delivery of the three listed artifacts (process map, prioritized backlog, and implementation recommendation).

## How to Proceed
To move forward, we request the following from the sponsor:
1. **Approve** this proposal and the associated commercial terms.
2. **Sign** the engagement agreement.
3. **Designate** a named client contact for scheduling and coordination.
4. **Remit** the initial 50% payment ($8,000) at kickoff to confirm the agreed start date.

Upon receipt of signature, named contact, and kickoff payment, we will confirm the start date and begin scheduling interviews and document collection.

We appreciate the opportunity to support this initiative and welcome any questions before proceeding.
