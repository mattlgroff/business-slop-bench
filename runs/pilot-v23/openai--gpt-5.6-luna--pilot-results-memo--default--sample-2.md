# Executive Readout: Pilot Scale Decision

## Recommendation

**Do not scale the pilot now.** Authorize a matched-ticket follow-up before making a scale decision.

## What the Results Support

- Mean handling time declined from **15 minutes at baseline** to **12 minutes during the pilot** across **200 baseline tickets** and **100 pilot tickets**.
- This result is **directional, not conclusive**: ticket mix differed, and allocation was not randomized.
- Quality performance declined from a **94% baseline pass rate** to **89% in the pilot**, below the required scaling threshold of **at least 92%**.
- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured, so the pilot has not yet demonstrated financial benefit.

## Decision Rationale

The pilot suggests a potential handling-time improvement, but it does not establish that the pilot caused the improvement. More importantly, observed quality is below the threshold required for scaling. The evidence therefore does not support immediate expansion.

## Next Decision

**COO decision required:** authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up.

The follow-up should determine whether handling-time improvement persists on comparable tickets while bringing quality to at least the **92% scaling threshold**. A later scale decision should be based on those results and measured staffing or cash impact.
