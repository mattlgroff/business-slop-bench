## Decision: Do not scale yet

The pilot’s mean handling time was 12 minutes, compared with 15 minutes at baseline. This supports a possible reduction in handling time, but the ticket mix differed and tickets were not randomly allocated, so the pilot does not establish that the process caused the reduction.

Quality fell from 94% to 89%, below the required 92% scaling threshold. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

Authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. The COO should decide whether to authorize that test. Reconsider scaling after results show whether handling time improves while quality meets the 92% threshold.
