# Discovery engagement proposal

## Purpose

We propose a four-week discovery engagement to provide:

- A current-state process map
- A prioritized backlog
- An implementation recommendation

These deliverables will give the sponsor a documented view of the current process, a ranked set of work items, and a recommendation for implementation.

## Commercial offer

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200 per hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment | 50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery |
| Duration | Four weeks |

Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded from this engagement.

The client will provide interview access and source documents. The sponsor will confirm acceptance when the three listed artifacts have been delivered.

## How to proceed

To authorize the engagement:

1. Sign the proposal.
2. Name the client contact.
3. Agree the start date.

Work will start after signature and confirmation of the named client contact.
