# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Objective and Scope

We propose a four-week discovery engagement to deliver:

1. A current-state process map  
2. A prioritized backlog  
3. An implementation recommendation  

Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Commercial Offer

The engagement comprises 80 hours at $200 per hour, for a total fee of **$16,000**.

- **50% at kickoff:** $8,000  
- **50% on delivery:** $8,000  

## Client Responsibilities

The client will provide:

- Access to interview participants  
- Relevant source documents  
- A named client contact  

## Acceptance

The engagement will be accepted when the sponsor confirms delivery of the three agreed artifacts:

- Current-state process map  
- Prioritized backlog  
- Implementation recommendation  

## How to Proceed

To proceed:

1. Approve and sign this proposal.  
2. Name the client contact.  
3. Agree the start date after signature and confirmation of the named contact.
