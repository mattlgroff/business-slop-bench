**Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Recommendation**

**Recommendation**
Do not scale the pilot at this time. 

**What the Results Support**
The current pilot results do not support a rollout, as the data is inconclusive and fails to meet required performance thresholds:
*   **Quality Failure:** The pilot achieved an 89% quality pass rate, which falls short of the minimum 92% threshold required for scaling. By comparison, the baseline pass rate was 94%.
*   **Inconclusive Speed Gains:** Mean handling time dropped from a 15-minute baseline (across 200 tickets) to 12 minutes during the pilot (across 100 tickets). However, this improvement is unreliable because the ticket allocation was not randomized and the ticket mix differed between the two groups.
*   **No Financial Impact:** No cash savings or staffing reductions have been measured from the pilot.

**Next Decision**
The COO must decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up to obtain accurate, comparable performance data.
