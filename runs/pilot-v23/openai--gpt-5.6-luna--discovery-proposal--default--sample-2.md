# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Objective and Scope

We propose a four-week discovery engagement to establish a clear basis for future decisions. The engagement will deliver:

1. A current-state process map  
2. A prioritized backlog  
3. An implementation recommendation  

Implementation activity is not included in this engagement.

## Commercial Offer

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200 per hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment | 50% at kickoff ($8,000); 50% on delivery ($8,000) |
| Duration | Four weeks |

The fee covers discovery activities and delivery of the three listed artifacts. Integrations, production support, implementation, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Client Inputs

The client will provide:

- Access to relevant interview participants
- Source documents
- A named client contact

## Start and Acceptance

The start date will be agreed after:

1. The proposal is signed; and  
2. The client has named its contact.

The engagement will be considered accepted when the sponsor confirms delivery of:

- The current-state process map
- The prioritized backlog
- The implementation recommendation

## How to Proceed

To authorize the engagement, the client sponsor should:

1. Sign this proposal.
2. Provide the name of the client contact.
3. Confirm access to interview participants and source documents.
4. Confirm the agreed start date.
5. Arrange the 50% kickoff payment of $8,000.

The remaining $8,000 will be due on delivery.
