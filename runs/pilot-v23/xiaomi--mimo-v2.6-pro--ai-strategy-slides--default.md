Slide 1: The Decision — Next Quarter's AI Pilot
- Quarterly pilot budget ceiling: $50,000.
- Team capacity allows only one pilot next quarter; nothing else can start alongside it.
- Two proposals compete for the slot: internal knowledge search vs. automated customer credit decisions.
- COO selects the pilot; results reported before any expansion.

Slide 2: Option A — Internal Knowledge Search ($30,000)
- Uses approved internal policy documents only; no new content approvals required.
- IT owner is Dana, who also reports results — clear accountability.
- Cost is $20,000 under the quarterly ceiling, leaving headroom.
- Success measured rigorously: answer accuracy on 100 reviewed questions, target at least 90%.
- No current result exists; the pilot is the first evidence point.

Slide 3: Option B — Automated Customer Credit Decisions ($45,000)
- Fits within the ceiling, but consumes 90% of quarterly budget.
- Legal approval is pending — gating dependency outside the team's control.
- Production use is not authorized, so real customer impact is off the table this quarter.
- Selecting B risks spending the quarter on a pilot that cannot proceed to use.
- Fewer defined success criteria than Option A.

Slide 4: Recommendation — Fund Option A
- Approved data, defined owner, and a measurable target create a faster path to credible results.
- Legal and authorization blockers make Option B unsuitable for a one-pilot quarter.
- Ask: COO selects the internal knowledge search pilot at $30,000.
- Dana reports accuracy results against the 90% target before any expansion.
- Option B can return for consideration once legal approval and authorization are in place.
