# Decision memo: Year-one vendor selection

**To:** CFO  
**Recommendation:** Approve Alpha for year one.

Alpha costs $92,000 in year one: $72,000 for the annual subscription and $20,000 for setup. This is $8,000 below the $100,000 budget ceiling. Alpha provides the required SSO and meets all other stated requirements.

Beta costs $70,000 in year one, saving $22,000 compared with Alpha. Beta cannot meet the mandatory production condition because SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date. Selecting Beta would prevent production deployment.

Approve Alpha and authorize Priya, the procurement owner, to proceed with signing.
