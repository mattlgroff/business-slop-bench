## Pilot scale decision

Recommend holding scale and authorizing a matched-ticket follow-up. The pilot’s 89% quality pass rate falls below the required 92% scaling threshold.

Mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets: an observed reduction of 3 minutes, or 20%. Ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the comparison does not establish that the pilot caused the reduction.

Quality fell from 94% to 89%, a decline of 5 percentage points. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured. The results support further testing of the handling-time improvement; they do not support scaling or claiming realized savings.

The next decision is for the COO to authorize Sam to run the matched-ticket follow-up. That test should assess whether lower handling time persists with comparable tickets and whether quality meets the 92% threshold. Use those results to reconsider scaling.
