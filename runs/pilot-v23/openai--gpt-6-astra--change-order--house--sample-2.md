# Change-order proposal: CRM connector

The client requests an additional CRM connector. The signed scope covers the reporting dashboard; the connector is outside that scope and is not included in the original fee.

The proposed fixed change fee is $7,000, based on an estimate of 40 hours at $175 per hour.

The connector adds an estimated two weeks to the schedule after both written sponsor approval and receipt of client-provided sandbox credentials. This is a schedule estimate, not an approved calendar date. Connector work will begin only after the sponsor approves in writing and the client supplies the credentials.

## Sponsor decision

Please approve or decline this proposal in writing.

- Approve: Authorize the CRM connector for a fixed change fee of $7,000 and accept the estimated two-week schedule addition, subject to credential receipt.
- Decline: Do not authorize the CRM connector; retain the existing signed reporting-dashboard scope.
