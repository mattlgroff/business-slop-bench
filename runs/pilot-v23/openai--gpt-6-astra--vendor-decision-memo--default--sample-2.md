# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**Decision requested:** Approve Alpha for year one at a total cost of **$92,000**, including setup.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Headroom under $100,000 ceiling | $8,000 | $30,000 |
| Mandatory SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Meets | Meets |

## Recommendation

**Choose Alpha.** It fits the year-one budget, including setup, and offers the SSO required before production. Both vendors meet the other stated requirements.

Beta costs $22,000 less in year one, but it cannot currently satisfy the mandatory SSO requirement. With no committed SSO delivery date, Beta is not a viable choice for production under the stated constraints. Its lower price does not offset that disqualification.

Alpha is therefore the only option that can meet the stated production requirements within the budget ceiling. SSO must be in place before production.

## Approval and Ownership

CFO approval is required before signing. Upon approval, **Priya, the procurement owner, should proceed with Alpha procurement** at the stated year-one total of $92,000.
