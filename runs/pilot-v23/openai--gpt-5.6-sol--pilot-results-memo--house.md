# Pilot scale decision

**Recommendation: Do not scale now. Authorize a matched-ticket follow-up.**

The pilot’s mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets. The results support further testing of a possible handling-time reduction.

They do not support scaling because the ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized. Quality also fell from a 94% pass rate at baseline to 89% in the pilot, below the required 92% scaling threshold. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

**Next decision:** The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run the matched-ticket follow-up. Scaling should return for consideration after that test measures handling time and quality on comparable tickets.
