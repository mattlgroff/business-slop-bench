# Decision memo: Year-one vendor selection

To: CFO

Approve Alpha for year one at $92,000, including setup. Alpha fits the $100,000 budget ceiling and offers the mandatory SSO capability.

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Amount below budget ceiling | $8,000 | $30,000 |
| Required SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Met | Met |

Beta costs $22,000 less in year one, but it cannot meet the requirement for SSO before production. With no committed SSO delivery date, there is no supported basis for planning a compliant production launch with Beta.

Alpha is the only vendor that can satisfy all stated requirements within the year-one budget. Its $22,000 premium over Beta buys access to a mandatory capability.

Priya owns procurement and should proceed with Alpha after CFO approval, which is required before signing. Production must remain conditional on SSO being in place.
