**Analysis**

Both vendors are within the $100,000 year-one budget ceiling, including setup. Alpha totals $92,000; Beta totals $70,000. Beta is cheaper by $22,000.

However, SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha’s required SSO is available. Beta’s required SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date. Both vendors meet the other stated requirements.

Because SSO is a mandatory production constraint, Beta does not satisfy the requirements for year one. Its lower price cannot compensate for the missing mandatory capability, especially without a committed delivery date. Selecting Beta would create an unresolved production blocker and potentially require additional cost, delay, or a separate solution not established in the source pack.

Alpha meets the mandatory SSO requirement and all other stated requirements. Its year-one total of $92,000 remains under the $100,000 ceiling, leaving $8,000 of budget headroom.

**Recommendation**

Approve Alpha for year one at $72,000 annual subscription plus $20,000 one-time setup, totaling $92,000. This satisfies the budget ceiling and the mandatory SSO requirement.

Beta should not be selected for year one unless SSO becomes available with a committed delivery date and all requirements are revalidated.

CFO approval is needed before signing. Priya owns procurement.
