# Pilot readout: do not scale now

The pilot reduced mean handling time from 15 minutes at baseline to 12 minutes across 100 tickets. This result supports a possible efficiency improvement, but ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the pilot cannot establish that the change caused the reduction.

Quality declined from a 94% pass rate at baseline to 89% in the pilot. The required scaling threshold is at least 92%, so the pilot does not meet the quality requirement. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

## Recommendation

Do not authorize broader scaling at this stage. The pilot provides an efficiency signal, but the quality result is below threshold and the evidence is not causal.

## Next decision

The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. That study should test handling time and quality on comparable tickets before any scale decision.
