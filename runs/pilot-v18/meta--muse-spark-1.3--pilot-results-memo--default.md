Executive Readout: Pilot Scale Decision

Recommendation: Do not scale the pilot now.

Handling Time:
Baseline: 200 tickets, 15 minutes mean handling time.
Pilot: 100 tickets, 12 minutes mean handling time.
Ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized.

Quality:
Quality pass rate was 94% at baseline and 89% in the pilot.
Required scaling threshold is at least 92%.

Costs:
No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

What Results Support:
Results support waiting, not scaling, because quality is below threshold and handling time comparison is confounded by differing mix and non-randomized allocation, with no measured savings.
