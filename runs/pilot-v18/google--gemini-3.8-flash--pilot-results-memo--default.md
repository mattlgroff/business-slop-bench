# Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Recommendation

### Recommendation
**Do not scale the pilot now.** 

### What the Results Support
The pilot results do not support scaling at this time due to unmet quality requirements, confounding variables, and an absence of financial return:

* **Quality Below Threshold:** The pilot achieved an 89% quality pass rate (down from 94% at baseline). This fails to meet the required scaling threshold of at least 92%.
* **Unvalidated Efficiency Gains:** Although mean handling time decreased from 15 minutes (across 200 baseline tickets) to 12 minutes (across 100 pilot tickets), allocation was not randomized and the ticket mix differed. As a result, the time reduction cannot be definitively attributed to process improvement.
* **No Measured Savings:** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured to date.
