# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Purpose

We propose a four-week discovery engagement to establish a clear basis for future implementation decisions.

## Scope and Deliverables

The engagement will produce:

1. A current-state process map  
2. A prioritized backlog  
3. An implementation recommendation  

## Commercial Offer

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200 per hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment | 50% at kickoff; 50% on delivery |
| Duration | Four weeks |

## Client Responsibilities

The client will provide:

- Access to interview participants
- Relevant source documents
- A named client contact

## Exclusions

The engagement covers discovery only. The following are excluded:

- Implementation
- Integrations
- Production support
- Guaranteed savings

## Acceptance

The sponsor will confirm delivery of the three listed artifacts: the current-state process map, prioritized backlog, and implementation recommendation.

## How to Proceed

To authorize the engagement:

1. Sign this proposal.
2. Nominate the client contact.
3. Agree the start date.

The start date will be agreed only after signature and confirmation of the named client contact. The first 50% payment is due at kickoff, with the remaining 50% due on delivery.
