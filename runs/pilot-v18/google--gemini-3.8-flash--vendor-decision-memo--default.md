**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)  
**FROM:** Priya, Procurement  
**SUBJECT:** Year-One Vendor Selection: Alpha vs. Beta  

### Purpose
To evaluate Vendor Alpha and Vendor Beta against our year-one constraints and obtain your required approval before signing.

### Constraint Evaluation

1. **Budget Ceiling ($100,000 maximum for year one, including setup):**
   - **Alpha:** $72,000 annual subscription + $20,000 one-time setup = **$92,000** (Compliant: $8,000 under budget).
   - **Beta:** $60,000 annual subscription + $10,000 setup = **$70,000** (Compliant: $30,000 under budget).

2. **Mandatory Single Sign-On (SSO before production):**
   - **Alpha:** Required SSO is available. (Compliant)
   - **Beta:** Required SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date. (Non-compliant)

3. **Other Requirements:**
   - Both vendors meet all other stated requirements.

### Analysis & Recommendation
Although Beta has a lower total cost ($70,000 vs. $92,000), it cannot be deployed because SSO is mandatory before production, and Beta offers no committed delivery date for this feature. Alpha fulfills the mandatory SSO requirement while remaining under the $100,000 year-one budget ceiling.

**Recommendation:** Approve selecting **Vendor Alpha** for year one. 

Please confirm your approval so procurement can proceed with signing.
