Pilot readout: scaling decision

Recommendation: Do not scale now.

Handling time averaged 12 minutes in the pilot on 100 tickets, compared with 15 minutes at baseline on 200 tickets. Ticket mix differed between the periods and allocation was not randomized.

Quality pass rate was 89% in the pilot, compared with 94% at baseline. The required threshold for scaling is at least 92%.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.
