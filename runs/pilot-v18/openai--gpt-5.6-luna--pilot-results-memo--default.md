# Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Recommendation

## Recommendation

**Do not scale the pilot now.** Authorize Sam to run the matched-ticket follow-up before making a scale decision.

## What the Results Support

- Mean handling time fell from **15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets** to **12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets**.
- However, the **ticket mix differed** and allocation **was not randomized**, so the pilot does not establish that the intervention caused the improvement.
- Quality declined from a **94% baseline pass rate** to **89% in the pilot**.
- The pilot therefore **missed the required scaling threshold of at least 92%**.
- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured, so the pilot has not yet demonstrated realized cost savings.

## Next Decision

**COO decision requested: authorize the matched-ticket follow-up.**

The follow-up should provide a more comparable test of handling time and quality before approving scale. A scale decision should be revisited after those results are available, with quality meeting or exceeding **92%** and evidence clarifying whether the handling-time improvement is attributable to the pilot.
