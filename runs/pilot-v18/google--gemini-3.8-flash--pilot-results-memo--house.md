# Pilot Scaling Recommendation

## Recommendation
Do not scale the pilot now. 

## Supported Results
The pilot data does not satisfy the requirements for expansion:

- Quality fell below the operating standard. Baseline quality pass rate was 94% across 200 tickets. The pilot achieved an 89% pass rate across 100 tickets, missing the required scaling threshold of at least 92%.
- Mean handling time decreased from 15 minutes at baseline to 12 minutes in the pilot. However, ticket allocation was not randomized, and ticket mix differed between the two groups. 
- The pilot measured no staffing reductions or cash savings.

The findings demonstrate a lower handling time alongside a decline in quality, but the lack of randomized allocation and the shift in ticket mix prevent confirming whether the pilot caused either outcome.
