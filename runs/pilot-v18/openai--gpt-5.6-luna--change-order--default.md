# Change-Order Proposal: Additional CRM Connector

## Requested Work
The client requests adding a CRM connector to the existing reporting-dashboard scope. This connector is not included in the original fee.

## Price
- Estimated effort: 40 hours  
- Rate: $175 per hour  
- Proposed fixed change fee: **$7,000**

## Schedule Effect
The connector is estimated to add **two weeks** to the schedule after both of the following occur:

1. Written sponsor approval is received.
2. Client provides sandbox credentials.

This is a schedule estimate, not an approved calendar date.

## Approval Requirement
The sponsor must approve this change in writing before connector work begins.

## Sponsor Decision

☐ **Approve** the additional CRM connector for the proposed fixed change fee of **$7,000**, with an estimated two-week schedule addition following written approval and receipt of sandbox credentials.

☐ **Decline** the requested change. The existing signed scope remains limited to the reporting dashboard.
