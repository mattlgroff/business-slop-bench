# Change-Order Proposal: Additional CRM Connector

## Requested Change
The client requests adding a CRM connector to the existing reporting dashboard scope. The CRM connector is not included in the original fee.

## Price
- Estimated effort: **40 hours**
- Rate: **$175 per hour**
- Proposed fixed change fee: **$7,000**

## Schedule Effect
The connector is estimated to add **two weeks** to the schedule. This estimate begins after both conditions are met:

1. The sponsor provides written approval.
2. The client provides sandbox credentials.

The two-week estimate is not an approved calendar date.

## Dependency
The client must provide sandbox credentials before connector work can begin.

## Approval Required
The sponsor must approve this change in writing before connector work begins.

## Decision

**Approve** the change order for the proposed fixed fee of **$7,000**, with an estimated schedule addition of **two weeks** after written approval and receipt of sandbox credentials.

**Decline** the change order. The additional CRM connector will not be added to the existing reporting dashboard scope.
