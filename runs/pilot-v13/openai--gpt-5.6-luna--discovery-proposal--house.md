# Discovery engagement proposal

## Purpose

Conduct a four-week discovery engagement to document the current state, prioritize the work backlog, and recommend an implementation approach.

## Deliverables

The engagement will produce:

- A current-state process map
- A prioritized backlog
- An implementation recommendation

## Commercial offer

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200 per hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment | 50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery |
| Duration | Four weeks |

## Client participation

The client will provide access to interview participants and relevant source documents.

## Exclusions

The fee covers discovery only. Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Acceptance

The sponsor will confirm delivery of the three listed artifacts.

## How to proceed

To approve the engagement:

1. Sign the proposal.
2. Name the client contact for the engagement.
3. Agree the start date after signature and contact designation.
4. Pay the 50% kickoff payment of $8,000.

The remaining $8,000 is due on delivery.
