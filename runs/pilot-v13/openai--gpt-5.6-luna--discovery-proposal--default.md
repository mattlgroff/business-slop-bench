# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Objective

Conduct a four-week discovery engagement to establish a clear basis for implementation decisions.

## Deliverables

The engagement will produce:

1. A current-state process map  
2. A prioritized backlog  
3. An implementation recommendation  

## Commercial Offer

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Duration | Four weeks |
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200 per hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment | 50% at kickoff ($8,000); 50% on delivery ($8,000) |

## Client Responsibilities

The client will provide:

- Access to relevant interview participants
- Required source documents
- A named client contact

## Scope Exclusions

The engagement excludes:

- Implementation
- Integrations
- Production support
- Guaranteed savings

## Acceptance

The sponsor will confirm delivery of the three listed artifacts: the current-state process map, prioritized backlog, and implementation recommendation.

## How to Proceed

To authorize the engagement:

1. Approve and sign this proposal.
2. Name the client contact.
3. Agree the start date.
4. Pay the 50% kickoff fee of $8,000.

The start date will be agreed only after signature and confirmation of the named client contact.
