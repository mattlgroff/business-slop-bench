# Pilot readout: do not scale now

The pilot reduced mean handling time from 15 minutes at baseline to 12 minutes across 100 tickets. The result does not establish that the pilot caused the improvement because ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized.

Quality declined from a 94% pass rate at baseline to 89% in the pilot. This falls below the required scaling threshold of 92%. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

**Recommendation:** Do not authorize broader scaling based on the current results.

| Finding | Decision relevance |
|---|---|
| Handling time fell by 3 minutes | Supports testing the approach further |
| Ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized | Limits causal confidence |
| Quality pass rate was 89% | Fails the 92% scaling threshold |
| No staffing reductions or cash savings measured | Provides no verified financial case |

**Next decision:** The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. That study should test handling time and quality on comparable tickets before any scaling decision.
