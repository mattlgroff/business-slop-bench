# Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Decision

## Recommendation

**Do not scale the pilot now.** Authorize a matched-ticket follow-up before making a scaling decision.

## What the results support

- Mean handling time decreased from **15 minutes at baseline** (200 tickets) to **12 minutes in the pilot** (100 tickets).
- This result does **not establish that the pilot caused the improvement**, because ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized.
- Quality declined from **94% at baseline** to **89% in the pilot**.
- The pilot is below the required scaling threshold of **at least 92% quality pass rate**.
- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

## Decision rationale

The pilot suggests a possible handling-time benefit, but the evidence is not causal and the observed quality result fails the scaling requirement. Scaling now would therefore be premature and could expand a process that has not demonstrated acceptable quality or measurable savings.

## Next decision

**COO decision required:** authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. The follow-up should provide a stronger basis for determining whether the handling-time result persists while meeting the **92% quality threshold**. Scale only after the follow-up supports both acceptable quality and a credible operational benefit.
