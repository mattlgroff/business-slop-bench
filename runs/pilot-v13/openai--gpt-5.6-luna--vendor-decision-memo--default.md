# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**From:** Priya, Procurement Owner  
**Subject:** Vendor recommendation for year one

## Recommendation

Approve **Alpha** for year one and authorize Priya to proceed with procurement, subject to CFO approval before signing.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| **Year-one total** | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Budget ceiling | Within $100,000 | Within $100,000 |
| Required SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Meets | Meets |

Both vendors are within the $100,000 year-one budget ceiling and meet the other stated requirements. Alpha’s year-one cost is $92,000, leaving $8,000 of budget capacity. Beta’s year-one cost is $70,000, leaving $30,000.

However, **SSO is mandatory before production**. Alpha has SSO available and therefore satisfies the stated production constraint. Beta does not have SSO available and has no committed delivery date, so it cannot currently satisfy the mandatory requirement.

## Decision

Select **Alpha**. Although Beta is $22,000 less expensive in year one, its lack of available SSO prevents production under the stated constraints. Alpha is the only vendor in the source pack that satisfies all requirements while remaining within budget. CFO approval is required before signing.
