

## Recommendation: Do Not Scale Yet

The pilot does not meet the conditions for scaling. Quality dropped to 89%, below the 92% threshold. No cost savings have been measured. The 12-minute handling time looks promising, but the pilot used a different ticket mix and randomization was absent, so we cannot attribute the time improvement to the intervention.

### What the results support

- The pilot generated data but not a reliable causal signal. Without randomization or matched controls, the 3-minute reduction in handling time is unexplained.
- Quality fell 5 points below baseline and 3 points below the required scaling gate.
- No staffing or cash savings exist yet.

### Next decision

Sam can run a matched-ticket follow-up that controls for ticket mix. The COO must authorize it. This follow-up would isolate whether the intervention itself produces the time savings observed in the pilot and whether quality can hold above 92% under comparable conditions. Scaling should wait for that result.
