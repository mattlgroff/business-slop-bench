

**Decision Memo: Vendor Selection for Year One**

**To:** CFO
**From:** Priya, Procurement
**Re:** Year-one vendor selection

**Recommendation: Alpha**

**Budget position**

| | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Under $100,000 ceiling | Yes | Yes |

Both vendors fit within the year-one budget. Beta saves $22,000.

**Constraint compliance**

SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha delivers SSO now. Beta has no SSO and no committed delivery date. Beta cannot enter production until SSO ships, and we have no timeline for when that happens.

**Recommendation rationale**

Beta's $22,000 savings carry an unbounded production delay. The constraint is binary: SSO must exist before we go live. Beta fails that constraint today and provides no committed fix date. Paying $70,000 for a vendor that cannot go to production is not a saving.

Alpha at $92,000 meets every stated requirement and stays under the ceiling. I recommend we proceed with Alpha.
