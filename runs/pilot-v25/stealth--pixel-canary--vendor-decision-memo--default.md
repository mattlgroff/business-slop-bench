

**Decision Memo: Vendor Selection for Year One**

**Prepared for:** CFO
**Prepared by:** Priya (Procurement)
**Subject:** Alpha vs. Beta

**Constraints:** SSO is mandatory before production. Year-one budget ceiling is $100,000 including setup.

| | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| **Year-one total** | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| SSO (mandatory) | Available | Unavailable, no committed date |
| Within budget | Yes | Yes |

**Evaluation:**

Both vendors fall within the $100,000 year-one ceiling and meet all non-SSO requirements. However, SSO is a mandatory gate before production. Beta cannot satisfy this constraint today and has no committed delivery date. Selecting Beta would block production deployment and expose the organization to indefinite delay.

Alpha meets every stated constraint: it is within budget at $92,000 and provides required SSO capability at contract start.

**Recommendation:**

Approve Alpha for year one at $92,000. It is the only option that satisfies the mandatory SSO requirement while remaining within budget.
