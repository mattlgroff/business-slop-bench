**Recommendation**

Approve **Alpha** at a year-one cost of **$92,000**. Alpha is the only vendor that meets every stated constraint.

---

**Evaluation Against Constraints**

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| **Year-one total** | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Within $100,000 ceiling | Yes ($8,000 headroom) | Yes ($30,000 headroom) |
| SSO (mandatory before production) | Available | Unavailable, no committed date |
| Other stated requirements | Met | Met |
| **Overall** | **Passes** | **Fails** |

---

**Rationale**

1. **SSO is a hard gate.** SSO is mandatory before production. Beta does not offer it and has not committed to a delivery date. Beta therefore cannot reach production in year one on any date we can plan around.

2. **Beta's savings cannot be realized.** Beta costs $22,000 less in year one. However, spending $70,000 on a vendor that cannot go to production buys no usable outcome. The lower price does not offset a failed mandatory requirement.

3. **Alpha fits the budget.** Alpha's $92,000 year-one total, including setup, is within the $100,000 ceiling.

4. **Otherwise equivalent.** Both vendors meet the other stated requirements, so SSO is the deciding factor.

---

**Risks and Considerations**

- **Limited budget headroom.** Alpha leaves $8,000 under the ceiling. Any cost beyond the quoted subscription and setup would reduce this margin.
- **Beta as a future option.** Beta could be reconsidered if it later commits to a firm SSO delivery date. This does not affect the year-one decision.
