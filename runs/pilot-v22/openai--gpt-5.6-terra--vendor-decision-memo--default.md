# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**From:** Procurement  
**Decision Required:** Approve vendor selection before contract signing.

## Recommendation

Approve **Alpha** for year one, subject to Priya completing procurement and presenting the agreement for CFO signature.

## Evaluation Against Constraints

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Within $100,000 ceiling | Yes | Yes |
| Required SSO before production | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Meets | Meets |

## Rationale

Both vendors fit within the $100,000 year-one budget ceiling, including setup. Beta is lower cost by $22,000 in year one. However, SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha has required SSO available, while Beta does not and has no committed delivery date.

Accordingly, Beta cannot satisfy the production requirement on the available facts, despite its lower price. Alpha is the only vendor that meets all stated constraints and remains $8,000 below the year-one budget ceiling.

## Requested Approval

Approve Alpha at a year-one cost of **$92,000** ($72,000 annual subscription plus $20,000 one-time setup). Priya should proceed with procurement; CFO approval is required before signing.
