# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO
**From:** Procurement (Priya, owner)
**Decision needed:** Approval to sign with Alpha

## Recommendation

Approve Alpha. It is the only vendor that meets the mandatory SSO requirement, and its year-one cost of $92,000 is within the $100,000 ceiling.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Within $100,000 ceiling | Yes ($8,000 headroom) | Yes ($30,000 headroom) |
| SSO available | Yes | No, with no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Met | Met |

## Reasoning

SSO is mandatory before production. Beta does not offer it and has not committed to a delivery date. Signing with Beta would leave us paying $70,000 for a system we cannot put into production, with no date to plan around. Its $22,000 cost advantage does not offset that.

Alpha meets every stated requirement. At $92,000, it uses 92% of the year-one budget. The remaining $8,000 is the margin for any unplanned year-one costs.

## Risks

- **Limited budget margin.** Any year-one cost above $8,000 beyond the quoted subscription and setup would exceed the ceiling. Priya should confirm with Alpha before signing that the quote covers all year-one charges.
- **Later years.** The source figures cover year one only. From year two, Alpha's subscription runs $12,000 more per year than Beta's, excluding setup. If Beta later delivers SSO, we can reassess at renewal.

## Next Steps

1. The CFO approves or rejects signing with Alpha.
2. On approval, Priya confirms that Alpha's quote covers all year-one costs and executes the contract.
