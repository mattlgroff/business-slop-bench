# Executive readout: Do not scale the pilot yet

**Recommendation:** Do not approve scaling now.

The pilot recorded a lower mean handling time: 12 minutes across 100 tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets. This supports further testing of a possible handling-time benefit.

The result does not support a scale decision. Pilot ticket mix differed from baseline, and allocation was not randomized, so the handling-time difference cannot be attributed to the pilot with confidence.

Quality also fell below the required scaling threshold. The pilot quality pass rate was 89%, versus 94% at baseline and a required minimum of 92%.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

**Next decision:** The COO should authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. Reassess scaling after that study measures handling time and quality against the 92% threshold.
