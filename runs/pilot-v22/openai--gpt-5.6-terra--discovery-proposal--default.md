# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Objective  
Conduct a four-week discovery to establish a clear foundation for a potential implementation.

## Scope and Deliverables  
The engagement will produce three artifacts:

1. Current-state process map  
2. Prioritized backlog  
3. Implementation recommendation  

## Commercial Offer  

| Item | Detail |
|---|---:|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200/hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment terms | 50% at kickoff; 50% on delivery |
| Duration | Four weeks |

## Client Responsibilities  
The client will provide access to interview participants and relevant source documents. A named client contact is required before the start date is agreed.

## Exclusions  
This engagement is limited to discovery. Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Acceptance  
Delivery is accepted when the sponsor confirms receipt of the current-state process map, prioritized backlog, and implementation recommendation.

## How to Proceed  
To approve the engagement, the client sponsor should confirm acceptance of this proposal and provide a named client contact. Following signature, the parties will agree the start date, and kickoff payment of $8,000 will be due. The remaining $8,000 is due on delivery of the three discovery artifacts.
