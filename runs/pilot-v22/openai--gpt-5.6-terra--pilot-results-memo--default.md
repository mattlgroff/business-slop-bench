# Executive Readout: Do Not Scale Pilot Yet

## Recommendation
Do not scale the pilot now. Authorize a matched-ticket follow-up before making a scale decision.

## Results
| Metric | Baseline | Pilot | Readout |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Tickets | 200 | 100 | Pilot sample was smaller |
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | 3-minute (20%) reduction |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | Below 92% scaling threshold |

## What the Results Support
The pilot supports further testing of a potential handling-time improvement: mean handling time fell from 15 to 12 minutes.

However, the result is not sufficient to support scaling because ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized. In addition, pilot quality was 89%, below the required scaling threshold of at least 92%.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

## Next Decision
The COO should authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. Reassess scaling after that follow-up confirms whether handling-time improvement can be achieved while meeting the 92% quality threshold.
