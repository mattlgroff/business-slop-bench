# Proposal: Discovery Engagement

**Prepared for:** Client Sponsor
**Purpose:** Request approval to begin a four-week discovery engagement

## 1. Objective

This engagement will establish a clear view of your current state and a practical path forward. It is limited to discovery, so you can make an informed decision before committing to any implementation work.

## 2. Scope and Deliverables

Over four weeks, we will produce three artifacts:

1. **Current-state process map:** a documented view of how the process operates today.
2. **Prioritized backlog:** identified opportunities, ranked in order of priority.
3. **Implementation recommendation:** our recommended approach for next steps.

## 3. Exclusions

The following are outside the scope of this engagement:

- Implementation
- Integrations
- Production support
- Guaranteed savings

Any follow-on work would require a separate agreement.

## 5. Client Responsibilities

To keep the engagement on schedule, the client will supply:

- Access to interview participants
- Relevant source documents

## 6. Acceptance

The engagement is complete when the sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts: the current-state process map, the prioritized backlog, and the implementation recommendation. The second payment is due on delivery.

## 7. How to Proceed

1. **Approve and sign** this proposal.
2. **Name a client contact** to coordinate interviews and document access.
3. **Agree a start date.** We will confirm this only after we receive both the signature and the named contact.
4. **Kickoff.** The first payment of $8,000 is due at kickoff, and the four-week discovery begins.
