Discovery Engagement Proposal

Purpose

This proposal asks the client sponsor to approve a four-week discovery engagement. The engagement will produce three artifacts that give the client a documented starting point for any later implementation decision.

Scope and deliverables

Over four weeks, we will deliver:

1. A current-state process map
2. A prioritized backlog
3. An implementation recommendation

The engagement covers discovery only. Implementation, integrations, production support and guaranteed savings are excluded. Any of these would require a separate agreement.

Client responsibilities

The client supplies interview access and the source documents needed to map current processes. Delays in access or documents may affect the four-week schedule.

Acceptance

The engagement is complete when the sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts. The second payment is due at that point.

How to proceed

1. The sponsor signs this proposal.
2. The client names a contact for the engagement.
3. We agree a start date with that contact. We set the start date only after both the signature and the named contact are in place.
4. The client pays the kickoff invoice of $8,000, and work begins.
