# Change Order Proposal: CRM Connector

**Requested work**
The client requests an additional CRM connector. The signed original scope covers only the reporting dashboard, so the connector is not included in the original fee.

**Price**
The proposed fixed change fee is $7,000. It is based on an estimate of 40 hours at $175 per hour.

**Schedule effect**
The connector adds two weeks after written approval and receipt of the client's sandbox credentials. This is an estimate, not an approved calendar date.

**Dependency**
The client must provide sandbox credentials before the two-week period begins.

**Sponsor decision**
The sponsor must approve this change order in writing before connector work begins. If the sponsor declines, the signed scope remains the reporting dashboard only.
