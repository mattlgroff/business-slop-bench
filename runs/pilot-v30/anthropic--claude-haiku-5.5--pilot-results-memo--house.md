**What the results support**

| Metric | Baseline (200 tickets) | Pilot (100 tickets) | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | None stated |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | At least 92% |

- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured, so the pilot does not yet support a financial case.

**Decision needed**

The COO decides whether to authorize Sam's matched-ticket follow-up. We recommend authorizing it. The follow-up would test whether the handling time gain holds on comparable tickets and whether quality can reach at least 92%.

Scaling should be reconsidered only if the matched-ticket follow-up shows both a handling time improvement and a quality pass rate of at least 92%.
