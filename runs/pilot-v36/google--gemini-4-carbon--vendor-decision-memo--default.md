**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Chief Financial Officer  
**FROM:** Procurement  
**SUBJECT:** Year-One Vendor Selection Decision  

**Executive Summary**  
We recommend selecting Vendor Alpha for year one. Alpha meets our $100,000 budget ceiling and satisfies all requirements, including mandatory Single Sign-On (SSO). Vendor Beta is disqualified due to lacking SSO.

** Evaluation Constraints**  
* **Budget:** Year-one ceiling of $100,000, including setup.  
* **Technical:** SSO is mandatory before production; both vendors meet all other stated requirements.  

**Vendor Comparison**

| Criteria | Vendor Alpha | Vendor Beta |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Annual Subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-Time Setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| **Year-One Total** | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Budget Compliance | Yes (Under $100,000 ceiling) | Yes (Under $100,000 ceiling) |
| Mandatory SSO | Available | Unavailable (no committed delivery date) |
| Other Requirements | Met | Met |

**Analysis**  
Vendor Beta offers a lower year-one total cost of $70,000 ($60,000 subscription plus $10,000 setup). However, SSO is mandatory before production, and Beta’s SSO is unavailable with no committed delivery date. Therefore, Beta cannot be deployed into production.

Vendor Alpha’s year-one total cost is $92,000 ($72,000 annual subscription plus $20,000 one-time setup), keeping us $8,000 beneath the $100,000 ceiling. Because Alpha provides the required SSO and meets all other stated requirements, it is the only viable option.

**Recommendation and Next Steps**  
We recommend approving Vendor Alpha at a year-one cost of $92,000.
