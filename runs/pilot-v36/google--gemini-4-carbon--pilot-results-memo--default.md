**What the Results Support**
The current pilot results do not support immediate scaling due to three critical limitations:
* **Missed Quality Threshold:** The pilot quality pass rate dropped to 89% from a 94% baseline, failing to meet the required scaling threshold of at least 92%.
* **Unverified Efficiency Gains:** While mean handling time decreased from 15 minutes at baseline to 12 minutes in the pilot, ticket allocation was not randomized and the ticket mix differed. Therefore, the results do not conclusively attribute the time reduction to the pilot itself rather than the differing ticket mix.
* **Unproven Cost Value:** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured during the pilot.

Because the data is confounded by a non-randomized, differing ticket mix and falls short of quality standards, scaling now introduces unacceptable quality risks without guaranteed efficiency or financial returns. However, the observed 12-minute handling time warrants controlled, isolated testing to determine if true efficiency gains exist when ticket types are properly aligned and quality is maintained.
