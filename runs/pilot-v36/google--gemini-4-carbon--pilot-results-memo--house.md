**Executive readout: Ticket handling pilot**

**Recommendation**
Do not scale the pilot now. Quality missed the required threshold, the test design confounded time results, and financial savings remain unmeasured.

**What the results support**
Mean handling time dropped from 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets to 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets. Because ticket mix differed between groups and ticket allocation lacked randomization, the data does not prove the pilot process caused the three-minute reduction.

Quality fell below the scaling standard. The pass rate dropped from 94% at baseline to 89% in the pilot, missing the required 92% minimum threshold. Scaling now risks degrading ticket quality across broader volume.

Cost impact is unknown. The team has measured zero staffing reductions or cash savings from the pilot. The current results support further controlled testing to isolate handling time and fix quality, once ticket mix is standardized.

**Next decision**
The COO must decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up test. Authorizing this test will isolate true handling-time changes on comparable work and show whether the pilot process can meet the 92% quality threshold before reconsidering scale.
