# Discovery engagement proposal

This proposal outlines a four-week discovery engagement for your approval. 

## Scope and boundaries

We will conduct four weeks of discovery work only. At the end of the engagement, we will deliver three artifacts:
- A current-state process map
- A prioritized backlog
- An implementation recommendation

Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded from this engagement.

Your team supplies interview access and source documents. As sponsor, you will confirm acceptance upon delivery of the three listed artifacts.

## Commercial offer

The engagement requires 80 hours at $200 per hour, for a total fee of $16,000. We bill in two installments:
- $8,000 (50%) at kickoff
- $8,000 (50%) on delivery

## How to proceed

To approve this engagement, sign this proposal and name your primary client contact. Once we have your signature and named contact, we will agree on a start date.
