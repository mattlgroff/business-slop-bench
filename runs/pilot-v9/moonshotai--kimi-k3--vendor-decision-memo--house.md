# Decision Memo: Vendor Selection, Year One

**To:** CFO
**From:** Priya, Procurement
**Re:** Vendor recommendation for approval before signing

## Recommendation

Select Alpha for year one at $92,000. Request CFO approval to proceed to contract.

## Cost against budget

The year-one ceiling is $100,000 including setup.

| Vendor | Subscription | Setup | Year-one total | Under budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha | $72,000 | $20,000 | $92,000 | Yes, by $8,000 |
| Beta | $60,000 | $10,000 | $70,000 | Yes, by $30,000 |

Both vendors fit the budget, so cost alone does not decide this.

## Constraint check

SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha has the required SSO available. Beta's SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date, so Beta cannot enter production in year one on any confirmed timeline. Both vendors meet the other stated requirements.

## Decision

Beta costs $22,000 less but fails a mandatory constraint. A signed contract with Beta would pay $70,000 for a system that cannot go into production until its SSO ships, with no committed date for that to happen. Alpha costs more and stays within the approved ceiling while meeting every stated requirement on day one.

The $8,000 budget headroom under Alpha remains available for any year-one needs.

## Requested action

CFO approval to sign with Alpha at $92,000 for year one. Once approved, I will execute the contract.
