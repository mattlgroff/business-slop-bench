# Change Order Proposal: CRM Connector

**To:** Client Sponsor
**Re:** Addition of CRM connector to the reporting dashboard project

## Requested work

The client has requested a CRM connector for the reporting dashboard. The signed scope covers the reporting dashboard only, so this connector requires a change order.

## Price

The connector is estimated at 40 hours at $175 per hour. We propose a fixed change fee of $7,000, added to the original fee.

## Schedule effect

The connector adds two weeks to the project. The two-week period begins after we receive both your written approval and the sandbox credentials. This two-week figure is an estimate, not an approved calendar date.

## Dependency

Work cannot begin until you provide sandbox credentials for the CRM. Delay in delivering credentials will delay the start of the two-week estimate.

## Approval

Your written approval is required before connector work begins. Please reply in writing with one of the following:

- **Approve:** Authorize the CRM connector at the fixed fee of $7,000 and the two-week schedule addition.
- **Decline:** The project proceeds under the existing signed scope for the reporting dashboard only, with no change to fee or schedule.

If you approve, please also indicate when the sandbox credentials will be available so we can confirm the estimated start date.
