# Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Decision

## Recommendation
**Do not scale now. Authorize Sam's matched-ticket follow-up.**

## What the Results Support

| Metric | Baseline | Pilot | Assessment |
|--------|----------|-------|------------|
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | 20% improvement, but confounded |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | **Below 92% threshold — fails** |
| Cash savings | — | None measured | No financial case established |

**Key limitations:** Ticket mix differed between groups; allocation was not randomized. The 3-minute handling time gain cannot be attributed to the intervention with confidence. Quality declined 5 points and missed the required scaling threshold.

## What the Results Do Not Support
- Causal claim that the pilot caused faster handling
- Readiness to scale based on quality performance
- Any ROI or cost-reduction justification

## Next Decision
**COO to authorize Sam's matched-ticket follow-up.** This addresses both confounds (ticket mix, non-random allocation) and tests whether quality recovers to ≥92% under controlled comparison.

**Decision gate:** Scale only if follow-up shows (1) quality ≥92%, (2) handling time improvement replicated on matched tickets, and (3) measurable cost impact identified.
