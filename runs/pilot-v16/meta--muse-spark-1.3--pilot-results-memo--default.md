Executive Readout: Pilot Scale Recommendation

Recommendation: Do not scale now.

Handling Time
Baseline: 200 tickets, 15 minutes mean handling time.
Pilot: 100 tickets, 12 minutes mean handling time.
Interpretation limited because ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized.

Quality
Baseline quality pass rate was 94%.
Pilot quality pass rate was 89%.
Required scaling threshold is at least 92%. Pilot does not meet threshold.

Costs
No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

What Results Support
Results support continued evaluation, not scaling. Lower mean handling time in pilot cannot support scaling due to differing mix and non-randomized allocation, quality is below threshold, and no savings measured.
