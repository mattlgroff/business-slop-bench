Pilot readout: hold on scale-up

Recommendation
Do not scale the pilot now. The results do not meet the scaling threshold and do not support a saving or speed claim.

Results
Mean handling time was 15 minutes at baseline on 200 tickets and 12 minutes in the pilot on 100 tickets. Ticket mix differed between the groups and allocation was not randomized, so the times cannot be compared as pilot effect.
Quality pass rate was 94% at baseline and 89% in the pilot. The required threshold for scaling is at least 92%. The pilot result is below it.
No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.
