**Executive Readout: Do Not Scale the Pilot Now**

**Recommendation:** Do not scale. Authorize Sam’s matched-ticket follow-up instead.

**What the results support:** The pilot handled 100 tickets at 12 minutes mean handling time versus a 200-ticket baseline at 15 minutes. However, ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the 3-minute improvement is not reliable evidence of a scalable effect.

Quality moved the wrong way against the scaling gate: baseline pass rate was 94%, pilot pass rate was 89%, and the required scaling threshold is at least 92%. The pilot therefore fails the stated quality requirement.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured, so there is no demonstrated financial basis to expand.

**Decision supported:** The current evidence supports continued testing, not scale-up. The apparent speed gain is confounded; quality is below threshold; economics are unproven.
