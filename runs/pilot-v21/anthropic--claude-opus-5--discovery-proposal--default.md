# Discovery Engagement Proposal

**Prepared for:** Client Sponsor
**Purpose:** Approval to commence a four-week discovery engagement

## Overview

We propose a focused, four-week discovery engagement to establish a clear, evidence-based view of your current state and a practical path forward. Discovery is deliberately scoped as an analysis and recommendation exercise, so you can make an implementation decision with confidence before committing further budget.

## Scope and Deliverables

The engagement covers discovery only and produces three artifacts:

| # | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| 1 | Current-state process map |
| 2 | Prioritized backlog |
| 3 | Implementation recommendation |

## What Is Excluded

To keep the commercial position unambiguous, the following are **not** included in this engagement: implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings. Any of these would be scoped and quoted separately.

## Commercial Offer

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200 per hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment terms | 50% at kickoff; 50% on delivery |

The fee is fixed against the scope described above.

## What We Need From You

The client supplies interview access and source documents. Timely access to the right people and materials is the single biggest driver of discovery quality, so we ask that this is confirmed before kickoff.

## Acceptance

Acceptance is straightforward: the sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts. No further acceptance criteria apply.

## How to Proceed

1. **Approve this proposal** and arrange signature.
2. **Name a client contact** who will coordinate interview access and source documents.
3. **Agree the start date.** The start date is agreed only after signature and a named client contact are in place.
4. **Kickoff**, at which point the first 50% invoice is issued.

## Recommendation

We recommend proceeding. For a defined $16,000 investment over four weeks, you receive a documented current state, a prioritized backlog, and a clear implementation recommendation — the inputs needed to decide whether, how, and in what order to invest in delivery.

Please confirm approval and nominate your named contact so we can agree a start date.
