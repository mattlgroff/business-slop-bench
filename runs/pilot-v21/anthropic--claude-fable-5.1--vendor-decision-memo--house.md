Decision memo: Year-one vendor selection

To: CFO
From: Priya, Procurement
Decision requested: Approve Alpha for year one.

Recommendation

Approve Alpha at $92,000 for year one. Beta is cheaper but cannot go to production, because SSO is mandatory before production and Beta does not offer it.

Cost against the $100,000 ceiling

| Vendor | Subscription | Setup | Year-one total | Under ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha | $72,000 | $20,000 | $92,000 | Yes, by $8,000 |
| Beta | $60,000 | $10,000 | $70,000 | Yes, by $30,000 |

Both fit the budget. Cost alone does not decide this.

Mandatory constraint

SSO is required before production. Alpha has it. Beta does not, and has given no committed delivery date. Signing Beta would mean paying $70,000 for a system we cannot put into production on any known date. The $22,000 saving is unusable if the product never reaches production in year one.

Both vendors meet the other stated requirements, so SSO is the only requirement separating them.

Risks with Alpha

Alpha leaves $8,000 of headroom under the ceiling. The source pack lists no other year-one costs, but any overrun above $8,000 would breach the budget. Procurement will confirm with Alpha before signing that $92,000 covers all year-one charges.

Conditions to revisit

Beta becomes viable only if it commits to an SSO delivery date that falls before our production date and puts that commitment in the contract. Without a date, Beta is not a year-one option.

Next steps

1. CFO approves Alpha at $92,000.
2. Priya confirms the all-in year-one figure with Alpha in writing.
3. Priya signs after CFO approval.
