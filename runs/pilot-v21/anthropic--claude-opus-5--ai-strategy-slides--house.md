## Slide 1: The decision

- One pilot can run next quarter; the team has capacity for a single project.
- Quarterly pilot budget ceiling is $50,000.
- Two candidates: internal knowledge search ($30,000) and automated customer credit decisions ($45,000).
- COO selects the pilot. Recommendation: internal knowledge search.

## Slide 2: Why knowledge search is the runnable option

- Cost is $30,000, leaving $20,000 of the ceiling uncommitted.
- It draws on approved internal policy documents, so no new content approval is needed.
- Dana in IT owns delivery and reports results before any expansion.
- Success is measurable now: answer accuracy across 100 reviewed questions, with a target of at least 90%.

## Slide 3: Why credit decisions cannot start next quarter

- Legal approval is pending, and production use is not authorized.
- At $45,000, it would consume most of the quarterly ceiling while blocked from production.
- Running it would use the team's only pilot slot on work that cannot reach customers.
- Option remains available for a later quarter once Legal approves.

## Slide 4: What happens if the COO approves

- Dana runs the knowledge search pilot at $30,000 against approved internal policy documents.
- Measurement: accuracy scored on 100 reviewed questions; no result exists today, so this becomes the first data point.
- Decision gate: 90% or higher supports a case for expansion; below that, Dana reports the gap before further spend.
- No expansion proceeds until Dana delivers results to the COO.
