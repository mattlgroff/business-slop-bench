# Executive Readout: AI Ticket-Handling Pilot

## Recommendation
Do not scale now. Authorize Sam's matched-ticket follow-up to produce comparable evidence.

## What the pilot showed
| Metric | Baseline | Pilot | Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tickets | 200 | 100 | n/a |
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | n/a |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | ≥92% |

## What the results support
- Handling time fell from 15 to 12 minutes, but the pilot's ticket mix differed from baseline and allocation was not randomized. The 3-minute gap may reflect easier tickets, not better performance.
- The pilot's 89% quality pass rate is below the 92% scaling threshold and below the 94% baseline.
- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured, so the time reduction has no confirmed financial return.

## What the results do not support
Scaling now would rest on a speed gain of uncertain cause, a quality result that fails the stated threshold, and no measured savings.

## Next decision
The COO decides whether to authorize Sam's matched-ticket follow-up. That study would compare like tickets across conditions, isolating the tool's effect on handling time and testing whether quality can reach 92% or better.

## Decision criteria for the follow-up
Proceed to scale if the matched study confirms a handling-time reduction on comparable tickets and quality meets or exceeds 92%. If quality stays below threshold, stop or redesign before any expansion.
