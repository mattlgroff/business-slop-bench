# Proposal: Discovery Engagement

**Prepared for:** Client Sponsor
**Purpose:** Approval of a four-week discovery engagement

## Overview

We propose a four-week, discovery-only engagement to establish a clear foundation for future improvement decisions. This engagement delivers three artifacts:

1. **Current-state process map**
2. **Prioritized backlog**
3. **Implementation recommendation**

## Commercial Offer

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Duration | Four weeks |
| Effort | 80 hours at $200/hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment terms | 50% at kickoff, 50% on delivery |

## Scope Exclusions

To keep the engagement focused, the following are explicitly excluded:

- Implementation
- Integrations
- Production support
- Guaranteed savings

Any of these can be considered separately after discovery concludes.

## Client Responsibilities

To enable timely delivery, the client will supply:

- Interview access to relevant personnel
- Source documents

## Acceptance Criteria

The engagement is complete when the sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts: the current-state process map, the prioritized backlog, and the implementation recommendation.

1. **Sign the agreement.**
2. **Name a client contact** to coordinate interviews and document access.
3. **Agree a start date** — the start date is set only after signature and a named client contact are in place.
4. **Kickoff payment** — 50% ($8,000) is due at kickoff; the remaining 50% ($8,000) on delivery.
