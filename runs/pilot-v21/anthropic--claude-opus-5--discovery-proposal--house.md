# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## What we will do

Over four weeks we will run a discovery engagement covering three deliverables:

1. A current-state process map
2. A prioritized backlog
3. An implementation recommendation

## What is not included

Implementation, integrations, production support and guaranteed savings sit outside this engagement. If you want any of them, we would scope and price them separately after discovery.

## Fee and payment

The engagement is 80 hours at $200 per hour, for a total of $16,000. We invoice 50% ($8,000) at kickoff and 50% ($8,000) on delivery of the three artifacts.

## What we need from you

Your team supplies interview access to the people who run the process today, plus the source documents we need to map it. You also name a single contact for the engagement.

## Acceptance

The engagement is complete when you, as sponsor, confirm we have delivered the current-state process map, the prioritized backlog and the implementation recommendation.

## How to proceed

Sign the proposal and name your contact. We will then agree a start date with you; the start date is set only after both of those steps are done.
