# Decision Memo: Vendor Selection, Year One

**To:** CFO
**From:** Priya, Procurement
**Re:** Recommendation to select Vendor Alpha

## Recommendation

Approve Vendor Alpha for year one at a total cost of $92,000 ($72,000 subscription plus $20,000 one-time setup). This fits within the $100,000 year-one budget ceiling and meets the mandatory SSO requirement.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| Setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| SSO (mandatory) | Available | Unavailable, no committed delivery date |
| Other requirements | Met | Met |

## Rationale

SSO is required before production. Beta does not offer it and has given no delivery date, so Beta cannot go live regardless of price. Beta's $22,000 cost advantage buys a product we cannot deploy.

Alpha meets all stated requirements and comes in $8,000 under the budget ceiling.

## Risk if we chose Beta

Signing Beta would mean paying $70,000 for software blocked from production until an uncommitted feature ships. With no delivery date, we cannot plan a go-live or hold Beta accountable to a timeline.

1. CFO approves this recommendation.
2. Priya finalizes contract terms with Alpha and routes the agreement for CFO signature.
