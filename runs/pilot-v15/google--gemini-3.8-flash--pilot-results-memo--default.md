# Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Recommendation

## Recommendation: Do Not Scale Now
We recommend against scaling the pilot at this time. 

## What the Results Support
* **Handling Time:** Mean handling time decreased from 15 minutes (across 200 baseline tickets) to 12 minutes (across 100 pilot tickets). 
* **Quality Threshold Missed:** The quality pass rate dropped from 94% at baseline to 89% in the pilot. This fails to meet the mandatory scaling threshold of at least 92%.
* **Unmeasured Financial Impact:** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured to date.
* **Methodological Limitations:** The ticket mix differed between phases, and ticket allocation was not randomized, limiting the reliability of direct comparisons.

## Next Decision
* **Action:** Sam is prepared to run a matched-ticket follow-up to address the allocation and mix discrepancies.
* **Decision Maker:** The Chief Operating Officer (COO) must decide whether to authorize this follow-up study.
