# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Objective  
Conduct a four-week discovery engagement to establish a clear foundation for future implementation planning.

## Scope and Deliverables  
The engagement will produce three artifacts:

1. Current-state process map  
2. Prioritized backlog  
3. Implementation recommendation  

## Commercial Offer  

| Item | Commercial Terms |
|---|---:|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200 per hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment | 50% at kickoff; 50% on delivery |
| Duration | Four weeks |

## Client Responsibilities  
The client will provide access to interview participants and relevant source documents. A named client contact is required before the start date is agreed.

## Exclusions  
This engagement is limited to discovery. It excludes implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings.

## Acceptance  
The sponsor will confirm delivery of the current-state process map, prioritized backlog, and implementation recommendation.

## How to Proceed  
To approve and begin the engagement, the client sponsor should:

1. Confirm approval of this proposal and the $16,000 fee.  
2. Sign the engagement agreement.  
3. Name the client contact and arrange interview access and source documents.  
4. Agree the start date following signature and confirmation of the named contact.  

Upon kickoff, the initial 50% payment is due. The remaining 50% is due on delivery of the three agreed discovery artifacts.
