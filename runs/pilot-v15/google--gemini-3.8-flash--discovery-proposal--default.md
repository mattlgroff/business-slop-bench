# Discovery Engagement Proposal

### Overview and Scope
This engagement covers four weeks of discovery only. Deliverables are limited to:
* Current-state process map
* Prioritized backlog
* Implementation recommendation

*Exclusions:* Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded from this engagement.

### Commercial Offer
* **Effort & Rate:** 80 hours at $200/hour
* **Total Fee:** $16,000
* **Payment Terms:** 50% invoiced at kickoff and 50% on delivery

### Client Responsibilities and Acceptance
* **Client Obligations:** The client supplies interview access and source documents.
* **Acceptance:** Formal acceptance occurs when the sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts.

### How to Proceed
To approve and initiate the engagement:
1. Provide signature approving this proposal.
2. Provide a named client contact.

Please note that the start date will be agreed only after signature and the provision of a named client contact.
