# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Scope

A four-week discovery engagement covering:

- Current-state process map  
- Prioritized backlog  
- Implementation recommendation  

Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Commercial Offer

The engagement comprises 80 hours at $200 per hour, for a total fee of **$16,000**.

| Payment milestone | Amount |
|---|---:|
| 50% at kickoff | $8,000 |
| 50% on delivery | $8,000 |

## Client Responsibilities

The client will provide:

- Access to interview participants  
- Relevant source documents  
- A named client contact  

## Acceptance

The engagement will be accepted when the sponsor confirms delivery of all three agreed artifacts:

1. Current-state process map  
2. Prioritized backlog  
3. Implementation recommendation  

## How to Proceed

To proceed, the client sponsor should approve and sign this proposal and name the client contact. The start date will then be agreed.
