# Proposal: Discovery Engagement

## Scope

We will complete four weeks of discovery work covering three deliverables: a current-state process map, a prioritized backlog, and an implementation recommendation. The engagement ends when the sponsor confirms delivery of these three artifacts.

## Commercial terms

The fee covers 80 hours at $200 per hour, for a total of $16,000. Payment is split 50/50: $8,000 at kickoff and $8,000 on delivery of the three artifacts.

The following are outside this engagement: implementation, integrations, production support, and any guaranteed savings.

## Client responsibilities

We ask the client to supply interview access with relevant staff and the source documents needed to map current processes.

## Start conditions

Work begins only after two conditions are met: the engagement is signed, and the client names a sponsor-side contact. Once both are in place, we will agree a start date with that contact.

## How to proceed

To approve this engagement, please confirm the following in writing:

1. Approval of the scope, fee, and payment schedule described above.
2. The name of the client contact who will schedule interviews and supply documents.
3. Signature of the engagement letter.

On receipt of these items, we will contact your named sponsor to agree a start date and issue the first invoice. Delivery follows four weeks after kickoff, at which point you confirm acceptance of the three artifacts and we issue the final invoice.
