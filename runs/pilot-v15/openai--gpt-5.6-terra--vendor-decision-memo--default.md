# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**From:** Procurement  
**Decision Required:** Approve vendor selection before contract signing.

## Recommendation

Approve **Alpha** for year one, subject to Priya completing procurement and obtaining final approval before signing.

## Evaluation Against Constraints

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Year-one subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Total year-one cost | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Within $100,000 ceiling | Yes | Yes |
| Required SSO available before production | Yes | No |
| Meets all stated requirements | Yes | No |

## Rationale

Alpha is the only vendor that satisfies the mandatory production requirement: required SSO is available. Its $92,000 year-one cost, including setup, is within the $100,000 budget ceiling.

Beta is less expensive at $70,000 in year one, but it cannot meet the mandatory SSO requirement because SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date. Selecting Beta would therefore prevent compliant production use.

## Required Action

Approve Alpha as the year-one vendor. Priya should proceed with procurement; CFO approval is required before signing.
