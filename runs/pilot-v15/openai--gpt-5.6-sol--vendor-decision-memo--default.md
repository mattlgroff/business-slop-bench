# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**Subject:** Approval to select Alpha for year one

## Decision

Approve **Alpha** for year one and authorize Priya, the procurement owner, to proceed toward signing.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Within $100,000 ceiling | Yes—$8,000 remaining | Yes—$30,000 remaining |
| Mandatory SSO before production | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Meets | Meets |

## Recommendation

Select **Alpha**. It is the only vendor that both satisfies the mandatory SSO requirement and remains within the year-one budget ceiling, including setup.

Although Beta costs $22,000 less in year one, it cannot meet the stated production constraint because required SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date. Beta should therefore not be selected for year one.

**Approval requested:** CFO approval to select Alpha at a year-one cost of **$92,000**, after which Priya can proceed with procurement before signing.
