## Executive readout: Do not scale the pilot now

The pilot reduced mean handling time from 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets to 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets, a 20% decrease.

The result does not support scaling because pilot quality pass rate was 89%, below the required threshold of 92% and below the 94% baseline rate. Ticket mix differed between baseline and pilot, and allocation was not randomized, so the handling-time difference cannot be attributed to the pilot alone.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

**Next decision:** The COO should authorize a matched-ticket follow-up. Sam can run the follow-up to test handling time and quality on comparable tickets before a scaling decision.
