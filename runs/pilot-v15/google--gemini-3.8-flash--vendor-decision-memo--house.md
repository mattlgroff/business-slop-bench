### Recommendation

Approve signing with Vendor Alpha for year one. 

### Decision Analysis

Procurement evaluated Alpha and Beta against the year-one budget limit and technical requirements. The budget ceiling is $100,000, including setup. Single sign-on (SSO) is mandatory before production. Both vendors meet all other stated requirements. CFO approval is required before signing.

* **Alpha:** The total year-one cost is $92,000 ($72,000 annual subscription plus $20,000 one-time setup). This fits within the $100,000 budget ceiling with an $8,000 margin. Alpha has required SSO available immediately.
* **Beta:** The total year-one cost is $70,000 ($60,000 annual subscription plus $10,000 setup). While this is $30,000 below the budget ceiling, Beta cannot provide SSO. The feature is currently unavailable and has no committed delivery date.

Beta fails the mandatory production condition. Alpha fulfills the SSO requirement and complies with the year-one budget limit.
