# Executive Readout: Do Not Scale Yet

## Recommendation
Do not scale the pilot now. The results support a matched-ticket follow-up, not operational rollout.

## Results
| Metric | Baseline | Pilot | Readout |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Tickets | 200 | 100 | Pilot sample was smaller |
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | 3-minute (20%) reduction observed |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | Below the required ≥92% scaling threshold |
| Staffing/cash savings | — | None measured | No realized financial benefit demonstrated |

## Interpretation
The pilot showed lower mean handling time, but ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized. Therefore, the observed handling-time improvement cannot yet be attributed reliably to the pilot approach.

Quality performance is the immediate gating issue: the pilot’s 89% pass rate is below the required 92% threshold. In addition, no staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

## Next Decision
The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. That follow-up should determine whether handling-time improvement can be sustained while meeting the ≥92% quality threshold.
