# Executive Readout: Pilot Scale Decision

## Recommendation: Do Not Scale Now

The pilot does not meet the criteria to proceed to scale.

## Key Findings

| Metric | Baseline | Pilot | Threshold | Status |
|--------|----------|-------|-----------|--------|
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | — | Improved but unreliable |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | ≥ 92% | **Below threshold** |
| Cost savings | — | None measured | — | Not demonstrated |

## What the Results Support

The pilot shows a directional improvement in handling time. However, three issues prevent a scale decision:

1. **Quality gate failed.** Pass rate fell to 89%, below the required 92% threshold.
2. **Comparison is confounded.** Ticket mix differed between baseline and pilot, and allocation was not randomized. The 3-minute improvement cannot be attributed to the intervention with confidence.
3. **No cost benefit measured.** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been identified.
