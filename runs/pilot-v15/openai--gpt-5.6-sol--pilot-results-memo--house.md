# Pilot executive readout

**Recommendation:** Do not scale the pilot now. Authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up.

The pilot’s mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets. This supports further testing of a possible handling-time reduction. It does not support attributing the difference to the pilot because ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized.

Quality fell from a 94% pass rate at baseline to 89% in the pilot. The pilot therefore missed the required scaling threshold of at least 92%.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

**Next decision:** The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam’s matched-ticket follow-up. A scaling decision should follow those results, subject to meeting the quality threshold.
