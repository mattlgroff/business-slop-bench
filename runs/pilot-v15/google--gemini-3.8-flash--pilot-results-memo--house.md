# Pilot Scaling Readout

## Recommendation
Do not scale the pilot now. The pilot failed to meet the required quality threshold, and the data does not confirm operational savings.

## Results Analysis
The pilot results support a reduction in handling time under specific, non-random conditions, but they do not support broader rollout.

* Handling time: Mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets, compared to 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets. Ticket allocation was not randomized, and the ticket mix differed between the two groups.
* Quality: The pilot achieved an 89% quality pass rate, falling short of the 92% minimum threshold required for scaling. The baseline pass rate was 94%.
* Costs: No cash savings or staffing reductions have been measured.

## Next Decision
The COO must decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. This follow-up would test whether handling time and quality performance hold when ticket mix is controlled.
