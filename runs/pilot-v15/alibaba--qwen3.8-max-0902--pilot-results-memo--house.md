**Executive Readout: Pilot Scale Decision**

**Recommendation: Do not scale the pilot at this time.**

The pilot does not meet the conditions required for a scale decision.

**Results**

| Metric | Baseline | Pilot | Threshold |
|--------|----------|-------|-----------|
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | — |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | ≥92% |

The pilot shows a 3-minute reduction in mean handling time across 100 tickets. However, the quality pass rate fell to 89%, below the 92% scaling threshold. The handling-time improvement also cannot be attributed to the intervention with confidence because ticket mix differed between baseline and pilot groups and allocation was not randomized.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

**What the results support**

The results support running a controlled follow-up to determine whether the handling-time improvement holds when ticket mix is matched and allocation is randomized. They do not support scaling.

**Next decision**

Sam can run a matched-ticket follow-up. The COO needs to decide whether to authorize that follow-up.
