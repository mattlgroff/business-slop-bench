# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Purpose and scope

This four-week discovery engagement will produce:

- A current-state process map
- A prioritized backlog
- An implementation recommendation

The engagement covers discovery only. Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Commercial offer

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200 per hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment | 50% at kickoff ($8,000); 50% on delivery ($8,000) |

## Client responsibilities

The client will provide access to interview participants and source documents. The client will name a contact before the start date is agreed.

## Delivery and acceptance

At the end of the engagement, the sponsor will confirm delivery of the current-state process map, prioritized backlog, and implementation recommendation.

## How to proceed

To approve the engagement, the sponsor should sign this proposal and name the client contact. The parties will then agree the start date.
