# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Scope and Deliverables
This four-week discovery engagement covers three deliverables:
- Current-state process map
- Prioritized backlog
- Implementation recommendation

Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

- **Effort and Rate:** 80 hours at $200 per hour
- **Total Fee:** $16,000

## Client Commitments and Acceptance

- The sponsor confirms acceptance upon delivery of the three listed artifacts.
