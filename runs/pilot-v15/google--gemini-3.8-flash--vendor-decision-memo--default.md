**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Chief Financial Officer  
**FROM:** Priya, Procurement  
**SUBJECT:** Year-One Vendor Decision: Vendor Alpha vs. Vendor Beta  

### Purpose
This memo evaluates Vendor Alpha and Vendor Beta against our year-one constraints to obtain CFO approval before signing.

---

### Evaluation Against Constraints

1. **Mandatory SSO Requirement**
   * **Constraint:** SSO is mandatory before production. Both vendors meet the other stated requirements.
   * **Vendor Alpha:** Required SSO is available.
   * **Vendor Beta:** Required SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date.

2. **Budget Ceiling**
   * **Constraint:** The year-one budget ceiling is $100,000, including setup.
   * **Vendor Alpha:** $72,000 annual subscription + $20,000 one-time setup = **$92,000** (within budget).
   * **Vendor Beta:** $60,000 annual subscription + $10,000 setup = **$70,000** (within budget).

---

### Recommendation
I specifically recommend selecting **Vendor Alpha**. 

While Vendor Beta has a lower total cost ($70,000 vs. $92,000), it fails the non-negotiable operational constraint: SSO is mandatory before production, and Beta cannot provide it or commit to a delivery date. Vendor Alpha meets all stated requirements, provides the required SSO, and successfully complies with the $100,000 year-one budget ceiling.

Please provide your approval to proceed with signing the contract with Vendor Alpha.
