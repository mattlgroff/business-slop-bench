## Slide 1: Readiness decision

- Service is not ready for operational handoff.
- Acceptance requires both an approved runbook and a successful rollback rehearsal.
- The runbook is approved.
- The rollback rehearsal has not happened.

## Slide 2: Remaining acceptance gap

- A successful rollback rehearsal is the outstanding acceptance criterion.
- No role has accepted responsibility for organizing the rehearsal.
- The rehearsal needs an assigned organizer before acceptance can proceed.

## Slide 3: Ownership before and after acceptance

- Lee, engineering lead, owns defect fixes until acceptance.
- Jo, operations lead, owns incidents only after acceptance.
- Operational incident ownership therefore does not transfer to Jo before acceptance.

## Slide 4: Required steering actions

- Sponsor Pat assigns responsibility for organizing the rollback rehearsal.
- The assigned owner organizes the rehearsal.
- Pat approves acceptance after the rehearsal succeeds, since the runbook is already approved.
- Operational handoff follows acceptance; until then, Lee retains ownership of defect fixes and Jo does not own incidents.
