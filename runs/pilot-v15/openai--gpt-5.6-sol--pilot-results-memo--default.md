# Executive Readout: Do Not Scale Yet

## Recommendation
Do not scale the pilot now. Authorize a matched-ticket follow-up before making a scaling decision.

## What the Results Support
The pilot’s mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets. However, the ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the results do not establish that the pilot caused the lower handling time.

Quality declined from a 94% baseline pass rate to 89% in the pilot. The pilot therefore did not meet the required scaling threshold of at least 92%.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

## Next Decision
The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. That follow-up should inform a subsequent decision on whether scaling is warranted.
