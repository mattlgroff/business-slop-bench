# Decision memo: Year-one vendor selection

**To:** CFO  
**Recommendation:** Approve Alpha for year one.

Alpha costs $92,000 in year one: $72,000 for the annual subscription and $20,000 for setup. This is $8,000 below the $100,000 budget ceiling. Alpha also provides the required SSO, so it meets all stated requirements.

Beta costs $70,000 in year one, $22,000 less than Alpha. Beta cannot be approved for production because SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date. Since SSO is mandatory before production, Beta fails a stated constraint despite its lower cost.

Priya should proceed with procurement for Alpha after CFO approval and before signing.
