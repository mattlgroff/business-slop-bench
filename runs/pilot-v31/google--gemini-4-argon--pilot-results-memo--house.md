### Pilot scaling recommendation

**Recommendation: Do not scale the pilot now.**

The pilot missed the required quality standard, and design flaws prevent a valid speed comparison. We have also not measured any staffing reductions or cash savings.

#### What the results support

Mean handling time dropped from 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets to 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets. Because the ticket mix differed and ticket allocation was not randomized, the data does not prove that the pilot caused the 3-minute reduction.

Quality fell below our minimum requirement. The baseline quality pass rate was 94%. In the pilot, the pass rate dropped to 89%, missing the 92% threshold required to scale.

| Metric | Baseline | Pilot | Scaling threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ticket volume | 200 | 100 | Not specified |
| Mean handling time | 15 minutes | 12 minutes | Not specified |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | At least 92% |
| Staffing or cash savings | Not measured | Not measured | Not specified |

#### Next decision

The COO needs to decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up test. A matched test will isolate the handling-time effect and show whether the team can reach the 92% quality pass rate under controlled conditions.
