# Discovery Engagement Proposal

This proposal outlines the scope, commercial terms, and approval process for a four-week discovery engagement.

## Scope and deliverables

The engagement covers four weeks of discovery only. During this period, we will produce three artifacts:

- A current-state process map
- A prioritized backlog
- An implementation recommendation

Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded from this engagement.

## Commercial offer

The total fee is $16,000, calculated as 80 hours at $200 per hour.

Billing occurs in two installments:

- $8,000 (50%) at project kickoff
- $8,000 (50%) upon delivery of the artifacts

## Client responsibilities and acceptance

Your team supplies interview access and source documents needed for the discovery work. The engagement concludes when you, as the sponsor, confirm delivery of the three listed artifacts.

## How to proceed

To approve this engagement and schedule the work:

1. Sign this proposal.
2. Provide a named client contact.
3. Agree on the start date with us after signature and contact assignment.
