**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Chief Financial Officer  
**FROM:** Priya, Procurement  
**SUBJECT:** Year-One Vendor Selection Decision  

**Executive Summary**  
Procurement requests your approval to sign with Vendor Alpha for year one. Alpha is the only evaluated vendor that meets all mandatory technical and financial constraints. 

**Constraints**  
*   **Budget Ceiling:** $100,000 for year one, including setup.  
*   **Technical Requirement:** Single Sign-On (SSO) is mandatory before production.  
*   **Other Requirements:** Both vendors meet all other stated requirements.  

**Vendor Evaluation**  

| Criteria | Vendor Alpha | Vendor Beta |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Annual Subscription** | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| **One-Time Setup** | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| **Total Year-One Cost** | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| **Budget Compliance** | Yes ($8,000 under ceiling) | Yes ($30,000 under ceiling) |
| **Mandatory SSO** | Available | Unavailable (no committed delivery date) |
| **Other Requirements** | Met | Met |

**Analysis**  
*   **Vendor Alpha:** With a $72,000 annual subscription and a $20,000 one-time setup fee, Alpha’s total year-one cost is $92,000. This satisfies the $100,000 budget ceiling. Furthermore, Alpha provides the required SSO capability immediately, clearing the path for production.  
*   **Vendor Beta:** Beta offers a lower year-one cost of $70,000 ($60,000 annual subscription plus $10,000 setup). However, Beta does not have the required SSO available and has no committed delivery date for it. Because SSO is mandatory before production, Beta cannot be deployed.  

**Recommendation**  
I recommend selecting **Vendor Alpha** at a total year-one cost of $92,000. While Vendor Beta is $22,000 less expensive in year one, its lack of mandatory SSO disqualifies it from production use. Vendor Alpha meets all stated requirements while remaining within the $100,000 budget ceiling.

**Next Steps**  
CFO approval is needed before signing. Upon your approval, I will finalize the procurement process with Vendor Alpha.
