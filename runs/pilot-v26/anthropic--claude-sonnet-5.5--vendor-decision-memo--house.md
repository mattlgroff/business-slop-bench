**Decision Memo: Vendor Selection for Year One**

**To:** CFO
**From:** Procurement (Priya)
**Decision needed:** Approval to sign with Alpha

**Recommendation**

Approve Alpha. Beta cannot be used in production because it lacks SSO, which is mandatory before production, and it has no committed delivery date for the feature.

**Cost comparison against the $100,000 year-one ceiling**

| | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Headroom under ceiling | $8,000 | $30,000 |
| SSO available | Yes | No, no committed date |

**Assessment**

Alpha meets the SSO requirement and stays within budget at $92,000, leaving $8,000 of headroom. Both vendors meet the other stated requirements, so SSO is the deciding factor.

Beta costs $22,000 less in year one. That saving has no value if the product cannot go live. Because Beta has no committed SSO date, we could pay for a subscription we cannot deploy, and we would have no date on which to plan production use.

**Risks and conditions**

Alpha's headroom is $8,000. Any year-one cost beyond the quoted subscription and setup would need separate approval, since the source pack lists no other charges. Procurement should confirm in the contract that the quoted fees are the complete year-one cost.

**Next steps**

1. CFO approves Alpha at a year-one total of $92,000.
2. Priya negotiates and prepares the contract, confirming the fee total and SSO availability in writing.
3. Signing proceeds only after CFO approval.

If the CFO prefers Beta on price, the condition would be a written, dated SSO delivery commitment from Beta before signing. None exists today.
