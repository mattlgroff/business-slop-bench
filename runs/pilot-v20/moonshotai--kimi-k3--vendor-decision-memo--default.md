# Decision Memo: Vendor Selection for Year One

**To:** CFO
**From:** Priya, Procurement Owner
**Re:** Recommendation for year-one vendor selection

## Summary

I recommend **Vendor Alpha** for year one. Alpha is the only option that satisfies all stated constraints while remaining within the budget ceiling.

## Evaluation Against Constraints

**1. Budget ceiling: $100,000 year-one total, including setup**

| Vendor | Subscription | Setup | Year-One Total | Within Budget? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha | $72,000 | $20,000 | $92,000 | Yes ($8,000 headroom) |
| Beta | $60,000 | $10,000 | $70,000 | Yes ($30,000 headroom) |

Both vendors fit within the $100,000 ceiling.

**2. Mandatory SSO before production**

- Alpha: Required SSO is available.
- Beta: Required SSO is unavailable with no committed delivery date.

SSO is mandatory before production, so Beta cannot be deployed to production in year one (or on any known timeline). Committing to Beta means paying $70,000 for a product that cannot go live.

**3. Other requirements**

Both vendors meet all other stated requirements, so these do not differentiate the decision.

## Recommendation

**Select Vendor Alpha at a year-one cost of $92,000** ($72,000 subscription + $20,000 setup). Although Beta is $22,000 cheaper, it fails the mandatory SSO constraint and offers no committed delivery date, making the lower price irrelevant — a contract signed with Beta yields no production capability.

## Requested Approval

CFO approval is required before signing. I request approval to proceed with Vendor Alpha for year one at $92,000 total.
