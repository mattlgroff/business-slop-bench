**Executive readout: do not scale the pilot now**

**Recommendation:** Do not scale. Authorize a decision on a matched follow-up instead of expansion.

**What the results support**
- Mean handling time was 12 minutes on 100 pilot tickets versus 15 minutes on 200 baseline tickets.
- That time gap is not a reliable scale signal. Ticket mix differed, and allocation was not randomized.
- Quality pass rate was 89% in the pilot versus 94% at baseline. The required scaling threshold is at least 92%. The pilot misses it.
- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured, so cost benefit is unsupported.

**What the results do not support**
- They do not support scaling now on speed, quality, or savings.
- They do not show that the shorter handling time would hold on a comparable ticket mix.

**Next decision**
The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. Scaling should wait until that comparison exists and quality meets the 92% threshold, with savings still unproven until measured.
