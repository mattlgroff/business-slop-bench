**Decision memo: Year-one vendor selection**

**To:** CFO  
**From:** Procurement (owner: Priya)  
**Re:** Alpha vs. Beta — approval before signing  
**Recommendation:** Approve Alpha. Do not select Beta for year one.

**Constraints**
- Year-one ceiling is $100,000, including setup.
- SSO is mandatory before production.
- Both vendors meet the other stated requirements.
- CFO approval is required before signing. Priya owns procurement.

**Cost (year one, subscription plus setup)**

| Vendor | Subscription | Setup | Total | vs. ceiling |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Alpha | $72,000 | $20,000 | $92,000 | $8,000 under |
| Beta | $60,000 | $10,000 | $70,000 | $30,000 under |

**Evaluation**
Alpha is inside the ceiling and has required SSO available, so it can go to production under the stated rules.

Beta is cheaper and also inside the ceiling, but required SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date. SSO is mandatory before production, so Beta cannot be used in production in year one on the facts given. Meeting the other stated requirements does not cure that gap.

**Decision**
Select Alpha. Year-one commitment is $92,000, within the $100,000 ceiling. Withhold approval for Beta until SSO is available with a committed delivery date that supports production. Signing still requires CFO approval; Priya remains procurement owner.
