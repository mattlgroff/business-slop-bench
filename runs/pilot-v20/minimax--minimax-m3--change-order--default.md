**CHANGE ORDER REQUEST — CRM Connector**

**Project:** Reporting Dashboard Engagement
**Prepared for:** Client Sponsor
**Subject:** Additional CRM connector (outside original scope)

**1. Background**
The existing signed scope covers the reporting dashboard. The client has requested an additional CRM connector, which is not included in the original fee. This change order formally requests authorization for that work.

**2. Requested Work**
Design, build, test, and deploy a connector between the client's CRM and the reporting dashboard. Scope of effort is estimated at 40 hours of development and integration work, contingent on client-provided sandbox credentials.

**3. Price**

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Estimated effort | 40 hours |
| Blended rate | $175/hour |
| Proposed fixed change fee | $7,000 |
| Billing basis | Fixed fee, not time-and-materials |

The proposed fixed fee of $7,000 supersedes the hourly math for invoicing purposes and reflects the agreed scope estimate.

**4. Schedule Effect**
Approval of this change order adds approximately two weeks to the project timeline, measured from the later of (a) written sponsor approval and (b) receipt of client sandbox credentials. This is an estimate, not an approved calendar date; a firm delivery date will be issued after approval and credential provisioning.

**5. Client Dependency**
The client must provide sandbox credentials before connector development can begin. Work will not start and the two-week estimate will not commence until credentials are received.

**6. Authority & Decision Required**
Per engagement terms, the sponsor must approve this change order in writing before connector work begins. No work, scheduling, or credential commitment will be initiated without written approval.

**7. Sponsor Decision**

- [ ] **APPROVED** — proceed at fixed fee of $7,000, schedule adjusted as noted.
- [ ] **DECLINED** — retain original dashboard scope; connector not pursued.
