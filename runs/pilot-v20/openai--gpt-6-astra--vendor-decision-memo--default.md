# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**Decision requested:** Approve Alpha for year one at a total cost of **$92,000**, including setup.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Total year-one cost | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Within $100,000 budget ceiling | Yes; $8,000 remaining | Yes; $30,000 remaining |
| Mandatory SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Meets | Meets |

## Recommendation

**Select Alpha.** It is the only vendor that can satisfy the mandatory SSO requirement based on the stated availability, and its year-one cost is $8,000 below the budget ceiling.

Beta costs $22,000 less in year one, but its savings do not offset its failure to meet a mandatory production requirement. With SSO unavailable and no committed delivery date, Beta is not a viable choice for production under the stated constraints.

## Approval and Execution

CFO approval is required before signing. Upon approval, **Priya**, the procurement owner, should proceed with Alpha procurement. Confirm that SSO is in place before production.
