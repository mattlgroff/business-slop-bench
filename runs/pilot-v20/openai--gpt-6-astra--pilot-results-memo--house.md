## Recommendation: do not scale now

The pilot’s quality pass rate was 89%, below the required 92% scaling threshold and down 5 percentage points from the 94% baseline.

Mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets: an observed reduction of 3 minutes, or 20%. Ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the comparison does not establish that the pilot caused the reduction. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

The results support further testing of the handling-time improvement. They do not support scaling or a claim of realized savings.

## Next decision

The COO should authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. It should assess whether the handling-time improvement persists with comparable tickets and whether quality reaches at least 92%. Use those results to reconsider scaling; any claim of cash savings will require separate measurement.
