## Executive Readout: Do Not Scale Now

**Recommendation:** Do not scale the pilot now. Authorize a matched-ticket follow-up to assess whether the handling-time improvement holds for comparable tickets and quality meets the required threshold.

### What the results support
- **Observed handling time improved:** Mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets versus 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets—a 3-minute, or 20%, reduction.
- **The pilot does not establish causation:** Ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the observed reduction cannot confidently be attributed to the pilot.
- **Quality did not meet the scaling requirement:** The pilot’s 89% pass rate was below the required 92% threshold and 5 percentage points below baseline’s 94%.
- **Financial benefits remain unproven:** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured. Lower handling time should not be presented as realized cash savings.

### Next decision
**The COO should authorize Sam to run the matched-ticket follow-up, rather than authorize scaling.** Use that follow-up to assess handling time on comparable tickets and whether quality reaches at least 92%. Revisit the scaling decision with those results; the current evidence does not support scaling now.
