# Discovery Engagement Proposal

**For approval by the Client Sponsor**

## Purpose

This proposal seeks your approval for a four-week discovery engagement. The engagement will produce three deliverables:

1. **Current-state process map**
2. **Prioritized backlog**
3. **Implementation recommendation**

## Scope

The engagement covers discovery only, over four weeks. It does **not** include:

- Implementation
- Integrations
- Production support
- Guaranteed savings

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200/hour |
| **Total fee** | **$16,000** |
| Payment terms | 50% ($8,000) at kickoff; 50% ($8,000) on delivery |

## Client Responsibilities

To keep the engagement on schedule, the client will supply:

- Access to interviews with relevant personnel
- Source documents

## Acceptance Criteria

The engagement is accepted when the sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts: the current-state process map, the prioritized backlog, and the implementation recommendation.

## How to Proceed

1. **Sign the engagement agreement.** Work begins only after signature.
2. **Name a client contact.** A named client contact is required before the start date is agreed.
3. **Confirm the start date.** The start date will be agreed jointly once both conditions above are met.
4. **Pay the kickoff invoice.** The first 50% of the fee ($8,000) is due at kickoff.
5. **Provide access.** Arrange interview access and share source documents so discovery can begin.

On delivery of the three artifacts and your confirmation, the final 50% ($8,000) becomes payable.
