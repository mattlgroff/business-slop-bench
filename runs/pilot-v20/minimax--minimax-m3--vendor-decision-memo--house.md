DECISION MEMO: Vendor Selection for Year One
To: CFO
From: Procurement
Re: Alpha vs. Beta, year-one contract

Recommendation: Sign with Alpha.

Both bids fit the $100,000 year-one ceiling. Alpha totals $92,000 ($72,000 subscription plus $20,000 setup). Beta totals $70,000 ($60,000 subscription plus $10,000 setup). The deciding factor is the SSO constraint.

SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha meets it. The required capability is available on Alpha today. Beta does not meet it. Beta's required SSO is unavailable, and Beta has not committed to a delivery date. Without SSO, Beta cannot go to production. Accepting Beta would block production on a date Beta will not commit to, while saving $22,000 in year-one spend that sits unused until SSO ships. That trade fails the stated constraints: the saving does not offset an unscheduled production hold.

Cost and risk summary:

| Item | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Year-one total | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Budget ceiling ($100,000) | Within | Within |
| Required SSO for production | Available | Unavailable, no delivery date |
| Meets stated constraints | Yes | No |
| Blocks production | No | Yes |

Next steps:
1. CFO approves Alpha.
2. Priya finalizes terms and signs.
3. Setup begins; SSO gates production deployment.

Rejecting Beta is a constraint decision, not a cost decision. Both options are affordable; only Alpha clears the SSO gate before production.
