# Executive Readout: Pilot Scale Decision

## Bottom Line
Do not scale now. The pilot fails the required quality gate, and the handling-time gain is not yet causally credible.

## What the Results Support
- Pilot handled 100 tickets at a 12-minute mean handling time versus 15 minutes at baseline (200 tickets), a 20% directional reduction.
- Quality pass rate dropped to 89% in the pilot, below the 92% scaling threshold and 5 points under the 94% baseline.
- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.
- The efficiency signal is confounded: ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the MHT gap may reflect easier tickets rather than the intervention. Net: insufficient evidence to scale.

## Key Risks
- Selection bias undermines the MHT claim.
- Quality regression below threshold signals customer-outcome risk if scaled.
- Financial case is unproven.

## Next Decision
Recommend the COO authorize Sam's matched-ticket follow-up before any scale call. A matched design isolates the pilot's true effect on handling time and quality under like-for-like conditions.
