**Decision memo: Vendor selection for year one**

To: CFO
From: Priya, Procurement
Subject: Award recommendation, Alpha

**Recommendation**

Award the year-one contract to Alpha, pending your approval before signature.

**Constraint check**

SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha supports the required SSO. Beta does not, and has no committed delivery date for it. Beta therefore fails a hard requirement and cannot be cleared for production launch on any predictable schedule.

Both vendors meet all other stated requirements, so the decision turns on SSO availability and cost.

**Cost against the $100,000 year-one ceiling**

| Vendor | Subscription | Setup | Year-one total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha | $72,000 | $20,000 | $92,000 |
| Beta | $60,000 | $10,000 | $70,000 |

Alpha fits the ceiling with $8,000 of headroom. Beta costs $22,000 less but remains unusable for production until SSO ships, and there is no date for that.

**Why not Beta**

Beta's $22,000 savings would buy a system that cannot go into production use. The subscription would run while the team waits on an SSO delivery date the vendor has declined to commit to. If SSO arrives late or never, the savings are lost entirely and we would restart procurement, paying setup twice across two vendors.

**Risks with Alpha**

Alpha consumes most of the year-one budget ($92,000 of $100,000), leaving little room for overruns inside this line. That is a budget-management issue we can monitor, and it is preferable to signing a vendor that fails a mandatory requirement.

**Requested action**

Approve the Alpha contract at $92,000 year-one total, so I can sign before the end of the quarter. If you want Beta revisited, we can reopen evaluation once it commits to an SSO delivery date and we reprice against next year's budget.
