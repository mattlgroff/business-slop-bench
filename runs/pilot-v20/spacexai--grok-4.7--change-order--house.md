Change-order proposal: CRM connector

The signed scope covers the reporting dashboard. The client has requested a CRM connector. That work is outside the original fee.

Price

Schedule

Client dependency
The client must provide sandbox credentials. Work on the connector cannot start until those credentials arrive and the sponsor approves this change in writing.

Approval
Approve or decline this change in writing. Connector work begins only after written sponsor approval.
