# Discovery Engagement Proposal

**Prepared for: Client Sponsor**

## Purpose

We propose a four-week discovery engagement to assess your current-state processes and define a clear path forward. This proposal outlines the scope, commercial terms, and next steps for your approval.

## Scope of Work

Over four weeks, we will deliver three artifacts:

1. **Current-state process map** — documentation of existing processes based on interviews and source materials.
2. **Prioritized backlog** — a ranked list of improvement opportunities.
3. **Implementation recommendation** — our advised approach for execution.

## Exclusions

This engagement covers discovery only. The following are explicitly excluded:

- Implementation
- Integrations
- Production support
- Guaranteed savings

## Commercial Offer

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Effort | 80 hours at $200/hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment | 50% at kickoff; 50% on delivery |

## Client Responsibilities

To enable delivery, the client will provide:

- Access to staff for interviews
- Relevant source documents
- A named client contact (required before a start date is agreed)

## Acceptance

The engagement is complete when the sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts: the process map, the prioritized backlog, and the implementation recommendation.

## How to Proceed

1. **Review and sign** this proposal to authorize the engagement.
2. **Nominate a named client contact** — the start date will be agreed only after signature and contact nomination.
3. **Pay the kickoff invoice** (50%, $8,000) to begin.
4. **Facilitate access** to interviewees and documents during the four-week period.
5. **Confirm acceptance** upon delivery of the three artifacts, triggering the final payment (50%, $8,000).

We look forward to your approval and to beginning discovery.
