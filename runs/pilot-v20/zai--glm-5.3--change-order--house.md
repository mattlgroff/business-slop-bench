# Change-Order Proposal: CRM Connector

**To:** Client Sponsor
**Re:** Requested addition to the signed reporting dashboard scope

## Requested work

You have requested a CRM connector beyond the existing signed scope, which covers the reporting dashboard. The connector is additional work and is not included in the original fee. This proposal requests your written approval before connector work begins.

## Price

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Estimated effort | 40 hours at $175/hour |
| Proposed fee | $7,000 fixed change fee |

## Schedule effect

The connector adds two weeks to the schedule, counted from two trigger events: your written approval and your receipt of sandbox credentials to the delivery team. This two-week figure is an estimate; it does not commit to a specific calendar delivery date. Work cannot begin until both conditions are met, so the delivery clock starts when the later of the two occurs.

## Dependency on you

Work depends on sandbox credentials supplied by your organization. Please provide them at approval time so the two-week estimate can begin immediately.

## Approval

Connector work begins only after you approve this change order in writing. On approval, the $7,000 fee applies and the schedule extension takes effect as described above. If you decline, the signed reporting dashboard scope, fee, and schedule remain unchanged.

Please reply in writing with your decision. A signed reply or email confirmation from you, the sponsor, satisfies the approval requirement.
