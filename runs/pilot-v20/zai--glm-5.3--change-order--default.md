# Change-Order Proposal: CRM Connector

**To:** Client Sponsor
**Re:** Addition of CRM Connector to Reporting Dashboard Project

## Requested Work

You have requested an additional CRM connector for the reporting dashboard. The existing signed scope covers the reporting dashboard only; the CRM connector is not included in the original fee. This change order covers the design, build, and integration of the requested connector.

## Price

- Estimated effort: 40 hours at $175/hour
- **Proposed fixed change fee: $7,000**

This fee is specific to the CRM connector work and is separate from the original agreed fee for the reporting dashboard.

## Schedule Effect

The connector adds approximately **two weeks** to the project timeline, counted from two triggering events:

1. Written approval of this change order by the sponsor
2. Receipt of sandbox credentials from the client

Please note this two-week figure is an estimate, not an approved calendar date. A firm date can be confirmed once both approval and credentials are in hand.

## Dependencies

The connector work depends on the client providing **sandbox credentials**. Work cannot begin, and the schedule clock does not start, until these credentials are received.

## Approval Requirement

Sponsor approval in writing is required **before any connector work begins**. No work on the CRM connector will commence without your written sign-off.

## Decision Requested

Please choose one:

- **Approve** — sign below; we will begin once credentials are also received
- **Decline** — the project continues per the original signed scope (reporting dashboard only) with no change to fee or schedule
