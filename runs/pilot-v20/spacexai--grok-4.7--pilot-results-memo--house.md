Do not scale the pilot now. The results do not meet the conditions required for a scale decision.

Mean handling time was 12 minutes on 100 pilot tickets, compared with 15 minutes on 200 baseline tickets. Ticket mix differed, and allocation was not randomized, so the shorter time does not by itself show that the pilot method caused the change.

Quality does not support scaling. The pilot pass rate was 89%. Baseline was 94%. Scaling requires at least 92%.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.
