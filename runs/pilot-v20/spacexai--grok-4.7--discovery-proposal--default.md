# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Offer
We propose a four-week discovery engagement limited to three deliverables: a current-state process map, a prioritized backlog, and an implementation recommendation.

## Commercial terms
| Item | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| Effort and rate | 80 hours at $200/hour |
| Total fee | $16,000 |
| Payment | 50% at kickoff; 50% on delivery |

## Exclusions
Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Client responsibilities
The client supplies interview access and source documents.

## Start and acceptance
The start date is agreed only after signature and a named client contact. The sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts.

## How to proceed
1. Confirm this scope, fee, exclusions, and acceptance criteria.
2. Sign and name the client contact.
3. Agree the start date and pay 50% at kickoff.
4. Provide interview access and source documents.
5. Review the three artifacts and confirm delivery, triggering the remaining 50%.
