**Change-Order Proposal: CRM Connector**

**To:** Client sponsor  
**Subject:** Written approval requested for work outside the signed scope

**Requested work**  
The existing signed scope is the reporting dashboard. The client requests an additional CRM connector. That connector is not included in the original fee.

**Price**  
The estimate is 40 hours at $175 per hour, for a proposed fixed change fee of $7,000. This fee is separate from the original fee.

**Schedule effect**  
The connector adds two weeks after written approval and credential receipt. This is an estimate, not an approved calendar date.

**Dependency**  
The client must provide sandbox credentials. The two-week estimate starts only after both written approval and credential receipt.

**Authority**  
The sponsor must approve in writing before connector work begins. Approval of this proposal authorizes the CRM connector at the proposed fixed change fee of $7,000 and accepts the estimated two-week schedule effect. Decline leaves the signed scope limited to the reporting dashboard, with no connector work and no change fee.

**Decision**  
Approve or decline in writing. Work will not begin without written sponsor approval.
