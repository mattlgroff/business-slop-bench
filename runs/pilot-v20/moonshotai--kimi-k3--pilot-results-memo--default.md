# Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Decision

**Recommendation: Do not scale now.** Authorize Sam’s matched-ticket follow-up instead.

## What the results support
- The pilot shows **lower mean handling time**: 12 minutes on 100 tickets versus 15 minutes on 200 baseline tickets.
- The result **does not establish a causal or scalable effect**: ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized.
- Quality moved in the wrong direction: pass rate fell from **94% at baseline to 89% in pilot**, below the **92% required scaling threshold**.
- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured, so efficiency gains are not yet tied to financial benefit.

## Decision rationale
Scaling now would accept below-threshold quality and unvalidated operational impact. The apparent handling-time improvement is promising but confounded by mix and non-random assignment.

## Next decision
**COO decision:** authorize Sam to run the matched-ticket follow-up. Revisit scaling only after the follow-up confirms handling-time gains on comparable tickets, restores quality to at least **92%**, and identifies measurable staffing or cash impact.
