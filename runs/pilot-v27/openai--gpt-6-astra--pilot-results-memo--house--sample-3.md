## Pilot scaling decision

Do not scale now. The pilot’s quality pass rate was 89%, below the required 92% scaling threshold and down from 94% at baseline.

Mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets: an observed reduction of 3 minutes, or 20%. Ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the results do not establish that the pilot caused the reduction. They support further testing of the handling-time benefit. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

The next decision is whether the COO will authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. Recommend approval to test whether the handling-time benefit persists with comparable tickets and whether quality reaches at least 92%. Use those results to reconsider scaling; do not treat the observed time reduction as measured cash savings.
