# Decision memo: Year-one vendor selection

To: CFO

Approve Alpha at a year-one cost of $92,000, including setup. It is the only vendor that can meet the mandatory SSO requirement on the stated facts, and it is $8,000 below the $100,000 budget ceiling.

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Amount below budget ceiling | $8,000 | $30,000 |
| Required SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Meets | Meets |

Beta costs $22,000 less in year one, but its unavailable SSO prevents production use under the stated constraints. With no committed delivery date, there is no basis to plan a compliant production launch with Beta.

Priya owns procurement and should proceed with Alpha after CFO approval, which is required before signing. SSO must be in place before production.
