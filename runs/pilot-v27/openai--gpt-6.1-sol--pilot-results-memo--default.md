## Executive Readout: Do Not Scale Yet

**Recommendation:** Do not scale the pilot now. The pilot’s quality pass rate was **89%**, below the required **92%** scaling threshold.

**What the results support**
- **A promising handling-time signal:** Mean handling time fell from 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets to 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets—a 20% reduction.
- **Not a demonstrated causal improvement:** Ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the observed reduction cannot be confidently attributed to the pilot.
- **A quality concern:** The pass rate fell from 94% at baseline to 89% in the pilot, leaving the pilot three percentage points below the scaling threshold.
- **No demonstrated financial savings:** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured. Faster handling should not be presented as realized savings.

**Next decision:** The COO should authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up rather than approve scaling. That follow-up should test whether the handling-time improvement persists with comparable tickets and whether quality meets the 92% threshold.

Revisit scaling after the follow-up provides stronger evidence on handling time and confirms that the quality requirement is met.
