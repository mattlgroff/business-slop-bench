# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**Subject:** Approve Alpha for year one

## Recommendation
Approve **Alpha**, with Priya managing procurement. Alpha meets the mandatory SSO requirement and fits within the $100,000 year-one budget ceiling, including setup.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Total year-one cost | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Budget headroom | $8,000 | $30,000 |
| Mandatory SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Meets | Meets |

## Decision Rationale
Both vendors are within budget and meet the other stated requirements. Beta costs $22,000 less in year one, but it fails the mandatory requirement that SSO be available before production. With no committed SSO delivery date, Beta is not a viable selection under the stated constraints.

Alpha’s $92,000 year-one cost leaves $8,000 below the budget ceiling. Its available SSO makes it the only vendor that satisfies all stated selection requirements.

## Approval and Next Step
**CFO approval requested:** Authorize selection of Alpha at a year-one cost of $92,000. Priya owns procurement and should proceed with signing only after CFO approval. Mandatory SSO must be in place before production.
