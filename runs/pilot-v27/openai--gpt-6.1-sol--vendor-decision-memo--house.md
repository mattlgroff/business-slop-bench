# Decision memo: Year-one vendor selection

To: CFO  
Procurement owner: Priya

**Recommendation:** Approve Alpha for year one at a total cost of $92,000, including setup.

Alpha’s $72,000 annual subscription and $20,000 setup fee leave $8,000 under the $100,000 budget ceiling. Required SSO is available, and Alpha meets the other stated requirements.

Beta costs $70,000 for year one: a $60,000 subscription plus $10,000 setup. It is $22,000 cheaper than Alpha and leaves $30,000 under the budget ceiling. Beta’s required SSO is unavailable, with no committed delivery date. Because SSO is mandatory before production, Beta cannot currently meet the production requirement. Both vendors meet the other stated requirements.

Alpha is the only option that meets the stated requirements within the year-one budget.

**Approval and next step:** CFO approval is required before signing. Upon approval, Priya should proceed with Alpha procurement and confirm that SSO is in place before production.
