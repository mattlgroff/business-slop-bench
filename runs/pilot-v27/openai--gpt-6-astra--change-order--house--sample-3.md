The client requests an additional CRM connector. The existing signed scope covers the reporting dashboard; the connector is outside that scope and is not included in the original fee.

The proposed fixed change fee is **$7,000**, based on an estimate of 40 hours at $175 per hour.

The connector adds an estimated two weeks to the schedule after both written sponsor approval and receipt of client-provided sandbox credentials. This is a schedule estimate, not an approved calendar date. The client must provide the sandbox credentials.

Connector work will begin only after the sponsor approves the change in writing.

- **Approve:** Authorize the additional CRM connector for a fixed change fee of $7,000 and accept the estimated two-week schedule addition, subject to receipt of sandbox credentials.
