# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO  
**Decision requested:** Approve Alpha for year one, subject to required SSO being in place before production.

## Recommendation

Select **Alpha**. It is the only vendor that can meet the mandatory SSO requirement based on the source pack, and its $92,000 year-one cost is within the $100,000 budget ceiling.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Total year-one cost | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Headroom under budget ceiling | $8,000 | $30,000 |
| Required SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other stated requirements | Meets | Meets |
| Suitability for production | Can satisfy SSO prerequisite | Cannot currently satisfy SSO prerequisite |

## Rationale

Alpha costs $22,000 more than Beta in year one but remains within budget, including setup. Beta’s lower price does not offset its failure to meet a mandatory production requirement. With no committed SSO delivery date, Beta offers no committed path to satisfying that prerequisite.

The decision should therefore prioritize compliance with the stated constraints rather than the lowest price.

## Approval and Next Steps

Approve Alpha’s $92,000 year-one expenditure. Priya, the procurement owner, should proceed with procurement following CFO approval and ensure required SSO is in place before production. No signing should occur before CFO approval.
