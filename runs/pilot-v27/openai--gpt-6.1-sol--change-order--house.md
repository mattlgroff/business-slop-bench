# Change-order proposal: CRM connector

The client requests an additional CRM connector. The existing signed scope covers the reporting dashboard; the connector is outside that scope and is not included in the original fee.

The proposed fixed change fee is **$7,000**, based on an estimate of 40 hours at $175 per hour.

The client must provide sandbox credentials. The connector adds an estimated two weeks to the schedule after both written sponsor approval and receipt of those credentials. This estimate is not an approved calendar date.

Connector work will begin only after the sponsor approves in writing and the client supplies the sandbox credentials.

## Sponsor decision

Please approve or decline this change order in writing.

- **Approve:** Authorize the additional CRM connector for the fixed change fee of $7,000 and accept the estimated two-week schedule addition, subject to credential receipt.
- **Decline:** Do not authorize the connector work; retain the existing signed reporting-dashboard scope.
