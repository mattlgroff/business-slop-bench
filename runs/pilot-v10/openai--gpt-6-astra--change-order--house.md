# Change-order proposal: CRM connector

The client requests an additional CRM connector. The signed scope covers the reporting dashboard; the connector is outside that scope and is not included in the original fee.

The proposed fixed change fee is **$7,000**, based on an estimate of 40 hours at $175 per hour.

The connector adds an estimated two weeks to the schedule after both written sponsor approval and receipt of client-provided sandbox credentials. This is a schedule estimate, not an approved calendar date. Connector work will not begin before the sponsor approves in writing.

## Sponsor decision

Please approve or decline this change in writing:

- **Approve:** Authorize the additional CRM connector for a fixed change fee of $7,000 and accept the estimated two-week schedule effect, dependent on written approval and receipt of sandbox credentials.
- **Decline:** Do not authorize the connector change. The reporting dashboard remains the signed scope.
