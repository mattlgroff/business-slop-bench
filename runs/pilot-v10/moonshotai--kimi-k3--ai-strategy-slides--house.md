**Slide 1: Decision Required: Select One AI Pilot for Next Quarter**

- The team can run only one pilot next quarter.
- Quarterly pilot budget ceiling: $50,000.
- Two options are on the table: internal knowledge search ($30,000) or automated customer credit decisions ($45,000).
- The COO selects the pilot. Dana reports results before any expansion.

**Slide 2: Option A: Internal Knowledge Search**

- Cost: $30,000, leaving $20,000 of headroom under the budget ceiling.
- Uses approved internal policy documents as its source material.
- IT owner: Dana.
- Success is measurable: answer accuracy on 100 reviewed questions, with a target of at least 90%.
- No current result exists; the pilot establishes the baseline.

**Slide 3: Option B: Automated Customer Credit Decisions**

- Cost: $45,000, consuming nearly the full quarterly budget.
- Legal approval is pending.
- Production use is not authorized.
- Even a successful pilot cannot be applied to real credit decisions this quarter.

**Slide 4: Recommendation: Fund Knowledge Search**

- Choose Option A. It fits the budget, has a named owner, and works only with approved documents.
- Option B is blocked: legal approval is pending and production use is not authorized, so $45,000 buys a pilot with no path to use.
- Option A carries a clear pass/fail test: at least 90% accuracy on 100 reviewed questions.
- Dana reports results to the COO before any expansion decision.
