# Decision Memo: Year One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO
**From:** Priya, Procurement
**Subject:** Recommendation on Alpha and Beta proposals

We must select a vendor that fits the $100,000 first-year budget ceiling and includes mandatory SSO for production deployment. Alpha satisfies these requirements. Beta fails them.

## Financial and Compliance Evaluation

| Vendor | First-Year Cost | Budget Fit | SSO Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Alpha | $92,000 | Compliant | Available |
| Beta | $70,000 | Compliant | Unavailable |

Beta offers a $70,000 first-year cost ($60,000 annual subscription plus $10,000 one-time setup). Alpha requires $92,000 ($72,000 annual subscription plus $20,000 one-time setup). Both remain under the $100,000 limit.

SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha provides SSO. Beta lacks SSO and offers no committed delivery date.

## Recommendation

Sign with Alpha. Beta's missing SSO blocks production deployment. The $22,000 premium Alpha requires buys compliance with the mandatory SSO requirement.
