## Approval Requested
Approve a four-week discovery engagement for a total fee of **$16,000**.

The engagement covers discovery only and will deliver:
- A current-state process map
- A prioritized backlog
- An implementation recommendation

Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded.

## Commercial Offer
The fee is based on **80 hours at $200 per hour**, totaling **$16,000**, payable as follows:
- **50% ($8,000) at kickoff**
- **50% ($8,000) on delivery**

## Client Responsibilities and Acceptance
The client will supply interview access and source documents. Acceptance occurs when the sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts.

## How to Proceed
To proceed, approve and sign the proposal and name a client contact. The start date will be agreed only after signature and the client contact has been named.
