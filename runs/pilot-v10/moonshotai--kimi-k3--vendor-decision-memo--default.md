# Decision Memo: Year-One Vendor Selection

**To:** CFO
**From:** Priya, Procurement
**Re:** Vendor selection — Alpha vs. Beta

## Summary

Recommend **Alpha**. It is the only vendor that can go into production under the stated constraints, and its total year-one cost fits the budget.

## Options

| Item | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Budget ($100,000 incl. setup) | Compliant ($8,000 headroom) | Compliant |
| Required SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |
| Other requirements | Met | Met |

## Evaluation Against Constraints

1. **Budget:** Both vendors fall within the $100,000 year-one ceiling, including setup. Alpha totals $92,000; Beta totals $70,000. Budget is not the deciding factor.

2. **Mandatory SSO:** SSO is required before production. Alpha has SSO available now. Beta lacks SSO and has no committed delivery date, meaning Beta cannot enter production and there is no defined timeline on which it could. Selecting Beta would commit funds to a product we are prohibited from deploying.

3. **Other requirements:** Both vendors meet all other stated requirements, so neither gains an advantage here.

## Recommendation

**Select Alpha and request CFO approval to sign.** Alpha is the only option that satisfies the hard SSO constraint, and its $92,000 year-one cost is $8,000 under budget. Beta's $22,000 cost advantage is unrealizable, since without SSO it cannot be used in production at all — the savings purchase zero usable capability.

## Next Steps

1. CFO approval of Alpha selection.
2. Priya executes procurement and contracts Alpha ($72,000 subscription + $20,000 setup).
