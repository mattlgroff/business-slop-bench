## Pilot scale decision

Do not scale now. The pilot’s 89% quality pass rate falls below the required 92% scaling threshold.

Mean handling time was 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets: an observed reduction of 3 minutes, or 20%. Ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the comparison does not establish that the pilot caused the reduction.

Quality pass rate fell from 94% at baseline to 89% in the pilot, a 5-percentage-point decline. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured. The results support further testing of the handling-time benefit; they do not support scaling or a claim of realized savings.

The next decision is for the COO to authorize Sam’s matched-ticket follow-up. Recommend approval, with handling time and quality assessed on comparable tickets. Use those results to reconsider scaling, subject to meeting the quality threshold of at least 92%.
