# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## To: Client Sponsor

## Purpose

We propose a four-week discovery engagement. At the end, you will receive three artifacts:

1. A current-state process map
2. A prioritized backlog
3. An implementation recommendation

These give you a documented view of how work flows today, an ordered list of what to fix, and a recommended path for implementation.

## Commercial Offer

- Duration: Four weeks
- Effort: 80 hours at $200 per hour
- Total fee: $16,000
- Payment: 50% ($8,000) at kickoff, 50% ($8,000) on delivery

## Scope Boundaries

This engagement covers discovery only. The fee does not include:

- Implementation
- Integrations
- Production support
- Guaranteed savings

If you approve the recommendation, implementation would be priced and contracted separately.

## What We Need From You

Your team supplies two things:

- Access to staff for interviews
- Source documents describing current processes

You also name one client contact before we schedule a start date. This contact coordinates interview scheduling and document delivery.

## Acceptance

The engagement is complete when you, as sponsor, confirm delivery of the three artifacts listed above. There is no extended acceptance process; delivery of the artifacts closes the engagement and triggers the final invoice.

## How to Proceed

To begin:

1. Sign this proposal or the attached agreement.
2. Name your client contact.
3. We agree on a start date after signature and contact confirmation.
4. Pay the kickoff invoice of $8,000.
5. We start the four-week engagement.

Discovery ends with delivery of the process map, backlog, and recommendation, followed by the final $8,000 invoice.
