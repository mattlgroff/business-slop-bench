EXECUTIVE READOUT: PILOT SCALING DECISION

Recommendation: Do not scale now. Authorize the matched-ticket follow-up.

Results:

| Metric | Baseline | Pilot | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | Not specified |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | ≥92% |

The pilot reduced mean handling time by 3 minutes. That supports a possible efficiency gain, but the ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the gain is not yet attributable to the pilot design.

Quality fell from 94% to 89%, missing the 92% scaling threshold by 3 points. This is the binding constraint.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured. The cost basis for scaling is therefore unproven.

Next decision: Sam proposes a matched-ticket follow-up. The COO decides whether to authorize it.

If authorized, the follow-up should test whether the 12-minute handling time holds when ticket mix is controlled and whether quality can return to at least 92%. Scaling should be reconsidered only after that evidence is available.
