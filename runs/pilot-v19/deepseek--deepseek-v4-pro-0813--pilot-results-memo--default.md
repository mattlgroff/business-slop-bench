**EXECUTIVE READOUT**

**Subject:** Pilot scaling decision — ticket handling time vs. quality threshold

**Recommendation:** Do not scale now. Authorize a matched-ticket follow-up.

**What results support**

| Measure | Baseline | Pilot | Required |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tickets | 200 | 100 | — |
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | — |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | ≥92% |

The pilot shows a 3-minute reduction in mean handling time (15→12 minutes). However, the quality pass rate fell from 94% to 89%, missing the required ≥92% scaling threshold by 3 points.

**Key limitations**

- Ticket mix differed between baseline and pilot.
- Allocation was not randomized.
- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

These limitations mean the handling-time gain cannot yet be attributed cleanly to the intervention, and the quality decline is a blocking risk.

**Next decision**

The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. This follow-up would control for ticket mix and allocation, allowing a valid comparison on handling time and quality before any scaling decision.

If the COO authorizes the follow-up, the next readout should require: quality ≥92%, handling-time gain sustained, and measured cost or staffing impact. If the COO does not authorize it, the pilot remains non-scalable under current evidence.
