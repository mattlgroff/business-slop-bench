**Executive Readout: Pilot Scale Decision**

**Recommendation: Do not scale now.**

**What the results support**

The pilot showed a 20% reduction in mean handling time (12 min vs. 15 min baseline). However, three issues prevent a scale decision:

1. **Quality below threshold.** Pilot pass rate was 89%, below the required 92% scaling threshold. Baseline was 94%.
2. **Design limitations.** Allocation was not randomized and ticket mix differed between pilot and baseline, so the speed improvement cannot be reliably attributed to the intervention.
3. **No cost impact measured.** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been demonstrated.

**Next decision**

Sam can run a matched-ticket follow-up to address the design gap. The COO must decide whether to authorize that follow-up.
