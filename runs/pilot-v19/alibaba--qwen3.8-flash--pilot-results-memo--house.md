# Pilot Recommendation: Do Not Scale

**Recommendation**

Hold the pilot and run a matched-ticket follow-up. Do not scale the new process now.

**What the results support**

Handling time improved from 15 to 12 minutes, but that result is weak evidence. The pilot had 100 tickets and the baseline had 200, the ticket mix differed, and allocation was not randomized. The mix difference alone could explain the time drop without any process change.

Quality failed the scaling gate: 89% in the pilot against a required 94% baseline and 92% threshold.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured. The case for scaling rests on a time gain that the design cannot attribute to the process, plus a quality drop that breaches the stated bar.

**Next decision**

The COO decides whether to authorize a matched-ticket follow-up. Sam can run it. That design should randomize allocation and match ticket mix to close the attribution gap and re-test the quality pass rate against the 92% threshold before any scale decision.
