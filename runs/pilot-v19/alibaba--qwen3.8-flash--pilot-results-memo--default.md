# Executive Readout: Scale Decision

## Recommendation: Do NOT Scale Now

The pilot does not meet the conditions required for a scaling decision. Two of three evaluation criteria are unmet or unreliable.

## What the Results Support

| Criterion | Pilot Result | Verdict |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Quality | 89% vs. 92% required threshold | **Fails** |
| Handling time | 12 min vs. 15 min baseline | **Not credible** |
| Cost savings | None measured | **Unknown** |

**Quality:** The pilot produced an 89% pass rate, below the 92% scaling threshold and a 5-point drop from the 94% baseline.

**Handling time:** Although the pilot shows a 3-minute improvement, the comparison is confounded. Ticket mix differed between groups, and allocation was not randomized. We cannot attribute the time reduction to the intervention.

**Cost:** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured. The financial case is absent.

## Next Decision

**COO should authorize a matched-ticket follow-up.** Sam is prepared to run a randomized comparison with identical ticket mixes, which will yield a valid handling-time signal and clarify whether quality regression is caused by the intervention or by ticket-mix differences. Until that result arrives, the quality threshold is unmet and the scaling case is unsupported.
