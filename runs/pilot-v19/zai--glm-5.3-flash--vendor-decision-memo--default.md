# Decision Memo: Vendor Selection — Year One

**To:** CFO
**From:** Procurement
**Re:** Vendor A vs. Vendor B, Year One
**Decision required:** Approve vendor selection before Priya signs.

## Options

| Item | Vendor A | Vendor B |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| **Year-one total** | **$92,000** | **$70,000** |
| Required SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |

## Constraints
- Year-one budget ceiling: $100,000, including setup.
- SSO is mandatory before production.
- Both vendors meet all other stated requirements.

## Analysis
**Budget:** Both vendors fit within the $100,000 ceiling. Vendor A totals $92,000; Vendor B totals $70,000, leaving $30,000 vs. $8,000 of headroom respectively.

**SSO (gating constraint):** Vendor A's required SSO is available. Vendor B's SSO is unavailable with no committed delivery date, meaning production readiness cannot be achieved within a predictable timeline. Because SSO is mandatory before production, Vendor B presents an unbounded schedule risk regardless of price.

**Other requirements:** No differentiation; both vendors satisfy them.

## Recommendation
Select **Vendor A**.

Vendor B is cheaper by $22,000 in year one and is within budget, but the mandatory SSO requirement is a hard gate, and Vendor B offers no delivery date. Choosing Vendor B risks an indefinite delay in production with no committed remedy. Vendor A is SSO-ready, fits the budget with $8,000 of margin, and is the only vendor that can satisfy the gating constraint in year one.

**Requested action:** Approve Vendor A; Priya may proceed to signature upon CFO approval.
