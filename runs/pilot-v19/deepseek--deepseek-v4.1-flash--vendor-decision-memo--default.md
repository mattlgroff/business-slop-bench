**Decision Memo**

**To:** CFO  
**From:** Procurement  
**Re:** Year-One Vendor Selection  
**Owner:** Priya

**Recommendation:** Select Alpha.

**Constraint Check**

| Item | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Year-one cost | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Budget ceiling: $100,000 | Within | Within |
| SSO mandatory before production | Available | Unavailable, no committed date |
| Other requirements | Met | Met |

**Analysis**

Beta is cheaper by $22,000 in year one. However, SSO is mandatory before production. Beta’s required SSO is unavailable and has no committed delivery date. That is a disqualifying gap, not a price advantage. Choosing Beta would block production indefinitely or force an exception to a mandatory control.

Alpha costs $92,000: $72,000 subscription plus $20,000 setup. This is within the $100,000 year-one ceiling, including setup. Alpha’s required SSO is available, and Alpha meets the other stated requirements. Alpha therefore satisfies every stated constraint.

**Recommendation**

Approve Alpha for year one at $92,000. Authorize Priya to proceed to signature, subject to CFO approval before signing.

**Note:** Beta should not be selected unless the SSO requirement is formally waived or Beta commits to a delivery date that satisfies production timing. Neither condition is present in the source pack.
