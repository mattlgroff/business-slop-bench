Recommendation: do not scale the pilot now

Pilot handling time averaged 12 minutes across 100 tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 tickets at baseline. Ticket mix differed between the periods and allocation was not randomized, so the 3-minute difference does not show a pilot effect.

Quality pass rate was 89% in the pilot, down from 94% at baseline and below the required scaling threshold of at least 92%.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.
