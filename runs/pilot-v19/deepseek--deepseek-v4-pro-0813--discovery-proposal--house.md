Proposal: Discovery Engagement

**Commercial offer**

Client sponsor approves a four-week discovery engagement at a fixed scope: 80 hours at $200/hour, totaling $16,000. Payment is 50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery. The work ends with three artifacts: a current-state process map, a prioritized backlog, and an implementation recommendation.

Discovery does not include implementation, integrations, production support, or guaranteed savings.

**Delivery**

| Artifact | Description |
|---|---|
| Current-state process map | Documents the existing workflow and handoffs |
| Prioritized backlog | Lists improvement items ranked by business value |
| Implementation recommendation | Describes a proposed path for moving selected items forward |

**Client responsibilities**

The client supplies interview access to relevant staff and provides source documents needed to map the current process.

**Acceptance**

The sponsor confirms delivery when the three listed artifacts are received.

**How to proceed**

1. Sponsor approves this proposal by signature.
2. Client names a primary contact for scheduling interviews and document requests.
3. Both parties agree a start date.

Work begins at kickoff. The $8,000 initial payment is due at that time. Final acceptance triggers the remaining $8,000 payment.
