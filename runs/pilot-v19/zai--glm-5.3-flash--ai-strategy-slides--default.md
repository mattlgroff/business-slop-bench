**Lower risk, clear path to measurable value**

- Clear success metric: ≥90% accuracy on 100 reviewed questions
- IT ownership assigned (Dana) — accountability is already in place

**Higher cost, blocked by unresolved approvals**

**Approve the knowledge search pilot**

- Governed evaluation: Dana reports results before any expansion decision
- Concrete go/no-go target: ≥90% answer accuracy on 100 reviewed questions
- Keeps $20K in reserve for contingency or early follow-on work
- Credit-decision automation can be revisited once legal approval is secured
