# Operational Handoff Readiness: Steering Committee Decision Pack

## Slide 1: Readiness summary

The service is one of two acceptance criteria from being ready. The runbook is approved. The rollback rehearsal has not happened, so Operations lead Jo cannot yet own incidents. Acceptance requires both criteria, therefore handoff is blocked pending the rehearsal.

## Slide 2: Readiness criteria

| Criterion | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Approved runbook | Sponsor Pat approves | Met |
| Successful rollback rehearsal | Unassigned | Outstanding |

Engineering lead Lee retains defect fixes until acceptance. Jo owns incidents only after acceptance. Today, no defect-free handoff path exists for incidents.

## Slide 3: Required actions

- Sponsor Pat assigns responsibility for organizing the rollback rehearsal. No role currently owns it.
- The assigned owner schedules and runs the rehearsal and records the result.
- Sponsor Pat reviews the rehearsal outcome and, once both criteria are met, approves acceptance.
- Jo assumes incident ownership on the acceptance date; Lee's defect-fix ownership ends then.

## Slide 4: Decision requested

The committee decides on three points:

1. Confirm Sponsor Pat assigns rehearsal ownership today, naming the responsible role.
2. Agree the rehearsal date and the escalation path if the rehearsal fails.
3. Approve acceptance in principle, conditional on a successful rehearsal result.

Approving these lets Jo take incident ownership and ends Lee's defect-fix obligation on schedule.
