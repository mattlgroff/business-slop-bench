# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Objective
We propose a focused four-week discovery engagement to establish a clear roadmap for your operational improvement initiatives. The goal is to move from ambiguity to actionable strategy through three core deliverables:
1. A current-state process map.
2. A prioritized implementation backlog.
3. A final implementation recommendation.

This phase is designed strictly for diagnosis and planning.

## Commercial Offer
We offer a fixed-effort model based on 80 hours at a rate of $200 per hour.

| Item | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Fee** | $16,000 |
| **Payment Term** | 50% at kickoff, 50% on delivery |
| **Duration** | Four weeks |

### What Is Not Included
To ensure scope clarity, the following are explicitly excluded from this engagement:
*   Implementation of changes.
*   Technical integrations.
*   Production support.
*   Guaranteed financial savings.

## Client Requirements
Success depends on timely collaboration. You must provide:
*   Access to key personnel for interviews.
*   Relevant source documents and process records.

## How to Proceed
Follow these three steps to initiate the engagement:

1. **Approve and Sign**
   Accept this proposal to secure your spot in our delivery schedule.

2. **Identify Stakeholders**
   Provide a named client contact to coordinate scheduling and information flow.

3. **Confirm Kickoff**
   We agree on a start date only after signature and assignment of your contact. Payment of the initial 50% deposit triggers the kickoff meeting.

## Acceptance Criteria
The engagement is considered complete when you confirm the delivery of all three listed artifacts: the process map, the prioritized backlog, and the implementation recommendation. Final payment is due upon this confirmation.
