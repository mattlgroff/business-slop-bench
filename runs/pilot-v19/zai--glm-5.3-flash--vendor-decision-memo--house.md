# Decision Memo: Vendor Selection, Year One

**To:** CFO
**From:** Procurement (Priya, owner)
**Decision required:** Approve one vendor before signing.

## Comparison

| | Alpha | Beta |
|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |
| One-time setup | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| Year-one total | $92,000 | $70,000 |
| Required SSO | Available | Unavailable; no committed delivery date |

Both vendors meet all other stated requirements.

## Evaluation

Budget: both fit under the $100,000 ceiling. Alpha leaves $8,000 of headroom; Beta leaves $30,000.

SSO: SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha provides it now. Beta cannot, and has given no committed delivery date, so a Beta contract carries an unknown delay before production can start. Beta's $22,000 year-one saving is offset by the risk that the tool sits unused while the requirement blocks go-live.

## Recommendation

Approve Alpha for year one.

Alpha is the only vendor that satisfies the mandatory SSO constraint today, at $92,000 it stays within budget, and it avoids an indefinite production delay. Priya will proceed with contracting on CFO approval.

If Beta delivers SSO with a committed date, we can revisit at renewal, when Beta's lower subscription price would apply on equal footing.
