**Decision memo: Year-one vendor selection**

To: CFO
From: Priya, Procurement
Re: Vendor choice for year one

**Recommendation: Select Alpha.**

**Cost comparison**

| Vendor | Subscription | Setup | Year-one total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha | $72,000 | $20,000 | $92,000 |
| Beta | $60,000 | $10,000 | $70,000 |

Both totals fall under the $100,000 year-one ceiling, which includes setup. Alpha leaves $8,000 of headroom; Beta leaves $30,000.

**Constraint check**

SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha offers the required SSO. Beta does not, and has no committed delivery date for it. Both vendors meet the other stated requirements.

**Analysis**

Beta costs $22,000 less in year one, but it cannot enter production under the stated constraints. Signing Beta would mean paying $70,000 for a system that sits unusable until Beta delivers SSO on an uncommitted timeline. The savings are real only if production access is optional, and the constraints say it is not.

Alpha meets every stated requirement, including SSO, at a total cost $8,000 below the budget ceiling. The budget accommodates Alpha without any trade-off against the mandatory requirements.

**Decision requested**

Approve Alpha at $92,000 for year one. Once you approve, I will proceed with signing per procurement process.
