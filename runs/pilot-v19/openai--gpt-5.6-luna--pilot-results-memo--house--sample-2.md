# Executive readout: pilot scale decision

## Recommendation

Do not scale the pilot now.

## What the results support

The pilot recorded a mean handling time of 12 minutes across 100 tickets, compared with 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets. This is a 3-minute, or 20%, reduction.

The comparison does not establish that the pilot caused the reduction. Ticket mix differed, and allocation was not randomized.

Quality declined from a 94% pass rate at baseline to 89% during the pilot. The required scaling threshold is at least 92%, so the pilot did not meet the quality requirement.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

## Next decision

The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. That study should test whether the handling-time result holds for comparable tickets and whether quality reaches at least 92%. Scale approval should be revisited after those results.
