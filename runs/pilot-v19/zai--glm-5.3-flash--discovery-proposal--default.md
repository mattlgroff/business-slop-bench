# Proposal: Four-Week Discovery Engagement

## Objective
A four-week discovery engagement to establish a clear foundation for future decisions. The engagement delivers three artifacts only:

1. Current-state process map
2. Prioritized backlog
3. Implementation recommendation

## Commercial Offer
- **Effort and rate:** 80 hours at $200/hour
- **Total fee:** $16,000
- **Payment terms:** 50% ($8,000) at kickoff; 50% ($8,000) on delivery

## Exclusions
The following are explicitly excluded from this engagement:
- Implementation
- Integrations
- Production support
- Guaranteed savings

No outcomes beyond the three artifacts listed above are included.

## Client Responsibilities
To enable the work, the client will provide:
- Interview access to relevant staff
- Source documents required for analysis

## commencement
A start date will be agreed only after both conditions are met: (1) this proposal is signed, and (2) the client names a dedicated contact.

## Acceptance
The engagement is complete when the sponsor confirms delivery of the three listed artifacts (current-state process map, prioritized backlog, and implementation recommendation).

## How to Proceed
1. Review this proposal and confirm the scope, fee, and exclusions meet your expectations.
2. Sign and return the approval to authorize the engagement.
3. Nominate a named client contact so a start date can be agreed.
4. Arrange interview access and provide source documents ahead of kickoff.

Upon approval, we will schedule the kickoff, at which point the first payment of $8,000 falls due. The remaining $8,000 is invoiced on delivery and acceptance of the artifacts.

We look forward to your approval and to delivering a clear, actionable basis for your next steps.
