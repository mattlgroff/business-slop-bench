# Discovery Engagement Proposal

## Purpose

This proposal requests your approval of a four-week discovery engagement. The work will document how your current processes run and produce a costed recommendation for any follow-on implementation.

## What we will deliver

At the end of four weeks you will receive three artifacts:

1. A current-state process map
2. A prioritized backlog
3. An implementation recommendation

You approve delivery by confirming receipt of these three items. Discovery ends there. Implementation, integrations, production support, and any guaranteed savings fall outside this engagement and would be scoped separately if you choose to proceed.

## Commercial terms

The engagement is fixed at 80 hours at $200 per hour, a total of $16,000. Payment is split evenly: 50% ($8,000) at kickoff and 50% ($8,000) on delivery of the three artifacts.

## What we need from you

Two things:

- Named sponsor and interview access to your process owners
- Source documents covering the in-scope processes

The start date is agreed only after this proposal is signed and a named client contact is provided, so nominating that contact is the practical first step.

## Next steps

1. Confirm your approval and sign the engagement letter
2. Name the client contact who will coordinate interviews
3. We agree a start date and schedule the kickoff, at which point the first invoice is issued

If the scope or fee needs adjustment, tell me before signature and I will issue a revised proposal. Otherwise, sign and name your contact, and we can begin within two weeks of receipt.
