Pilot readout: Scale decision

Recommendation
Do not scale the pilot now. Results do not meet the quality threshold for scaling, and time and cost results do not support scale.

Results
Mean handling time was 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets and 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets. Ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the times are not directly comparable.
Quality pass rate was 94% at baseline and 89% in the pilot. The required threshold for scaling is at least 92%.
No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

Next decision
