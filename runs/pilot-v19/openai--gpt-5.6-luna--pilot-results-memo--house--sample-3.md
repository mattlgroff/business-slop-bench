# Executive readout: defer scaling

## Recommendation

Do not scale the pilot now. Authorize a matched-ticket follow-up before making a scaling decision.

## What the results support

| Measure | Baseline | Pilot | Readout |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Tickets | 200 | 100 | Pilot sample was smaller |
| Mean handling time | 15 minutes | 12 minutes | 3-minute lower average in the pilot |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | Pilot was below the 92% scaling threshold |
| Measured savings | None | None | No staffing reductions or cash savings measured |

The pilot shows a lower mean handling time, but ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized. The result therefore does not establish that the pilot caused the time reduction. Quality also fell below the required threshold, which alone does not support scale authorization.

## Next decision

The COO should decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. That study should provide a fairer comparison of handling time and quality before the company considers scaling.
