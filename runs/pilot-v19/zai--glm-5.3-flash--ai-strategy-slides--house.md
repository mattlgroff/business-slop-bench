# Slide 1: The Decision
**COO selects one pilot for next quarter**

- Team capacity supports one pilot, so we choose between two funded options
- Pilot budget ceiling: $50,000
- Dana reports results before any expansion

# Slide 2: Option A: Internal Knowledge Search Pilot
**Cost: $30,000, within budget**

- Uses approved internal policy documents, so no pending approvals block the start
- IT owner is Dana, an assigned and accountable lead
- Success is measurable: answer accuracy on 100 reviewed questions, with a target of at least 90%
- No current result exists, which makes the pilot the fastest way to establish one

# Slide 3: Option B: Automated Customer Credit Decisions
**Cost: $45,000, within budget, but blocked on approvals**

- Legal approval is pending and production use is not authorized
- The pilot cannot move to production even if it performs well this quarter
- $45,000 against a $50,000 ceiling leaves only $5,000 of the budget available for anything else
- Approving B risks spending the quarter's full capacity on a pilot that cannot ship

# Slide 4: Recommendation
**Fund the knowledge search pilot now**

- Option A starts immediately: documents are approved, Dana owns it, and the 90% accuracy target gives the COO a clear go/no-go number
- Option A costs $30,000, leaving $20,000 of the ceiling unspent
- Option B should wait for legal approval; once cleared, it can be a candidate for the following quarter
- Decision requested: COO approves Option A, with Dana reporting results before any expansion
