Approve Alpha. It is the only vendor that meets the mandatory SSO requirement, and it fits within the year-one budget.

|---|---|---|
| Annual subscription | $72,000 | $60,000 |

## Analysis

**Budget.** Both vendors fall under the $100,000 ceiling, including setup. Beta is $22,000 cheaper in year one.

**SSO.** SSO is mandatory before production. Alpha provides it today. Beta does not, and has given no committed delivery date. Selecting Beta would mean paying for a system that cannot enter production for an unknown period.

**Conclusion.** Beta's savings do not offset a failed mandatory constraint. Cost is a differentiator only between options that qualify, and Beta does not qualify. Alpha meets every stated requirement with $8,000 of headroom.

## Risks and Considerations

- **Alpha:** Higher cost, though within budget. The remaining headroom is $8,000, so any additional year-one costs should be confirmed before signing.
- **Beta:** Could be reconsidered in future if SSO is delivered and verified. An uncommitted roadmap item should not be relied upon for this decision.
