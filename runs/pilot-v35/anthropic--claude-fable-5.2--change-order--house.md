# Change-order proposal: CRM connector

To: Client sponsor

## Requested work

You asked us to add a CRM connector. The signed scope covers the reporting dashboard only, so the connector is outside the original fee and needs a change order.

## Price

We propose a fixed change fee of $7,000, based on an estimate of 40 hours at $175 per hour. This fee is in addition to the original fee.

## Schedule effect

The connector adds an estimated two weeks. That period starts once we have both your written approval and the sandbox credentials. Two weeks is an estimate; we have not set an approved calendar date.

## What we need from you

- Written approval of this change order. We will not begin connector work without it.
- Sandbox credentials for the CRM.

## Decision

Please reply in writing with one of the following:

- Approve: you accept the $7,000 fixed change fee and the estimated two-week addition. Connector work begins after we receive your approval and the credentials.
- Decline: the signed scope remains the reporting dashboard, with no change to the original fee.
