# Slide 1: One Pilot, One Decision

- The team can run only one AI pilot next quarter
- Quarterly pilot budget ceiling: $50,000
- Two candidates: internal knowledge search or automated customer credit decisions
- The COO selects the pilot
- Recommendation: internal knowledge search

# Slide 2: The Options Side by Side

| | Knowledge Search | Automated Credit Decisions |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | $30,000 | $45,000 |
| Budget left under ceiling | $20,000 | $5,000 |
| Readiness | Uses approved internal policy documents | Legal approval pending |
| Production status | Not yet evaluated | Production use not authorized |
| IT owner | Dana | None named |

- Both options fit within the ceiling
- Only knowledge search is clear to proceed today

# Slide 3: Why Knowledge Search Wins

- **Ready now:** built on internal policy documents that are already approved
- **Lower spend:** $30,000, which is $15,000 less than the credit option
- **Clear accountability:** Dana owns the pilot and reports the results
- **Measurable outcome:** answer accuracy on 100 reviewed questions, with a target of at least 90%
- **Credit decisions carry an open blocker:** Legal approval is pending and production use is not authorized
- Choosing the credit option commits the quarter's only pilot slot to work that cannot yet go to production

**Caveat:** no accuracy result exists yet. The 90% figure is a target, and the pilot is how we find out whether it is achievable.

# Slide 4: Decision Requested and Next Steps

**Decision for the COO:** select internal knowledge search as next quarter's pilot at $30,000.

If approved:

- Dana leads the pilot using approved internal policy documents
- Accuracy is evaluated on 100 reviewed questions against the 90% target
- Dana reports results before any expansion
- No expansion proceeds until those results are reported

Automated credit decisions:

- Not selected for next quarter
- Can be reconsidered for a future quarter once Legal approval is resolved
