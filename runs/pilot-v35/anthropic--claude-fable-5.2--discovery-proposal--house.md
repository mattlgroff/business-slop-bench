# Discovery engagement proposal

To: Client sponsor
Decision requested: approve a four-week discovery engagement at a fixed scope of 80 hours.

## What you will receive

We will spend four weeks on discovery and deliver three artifacts:

1. A current-state process map
2. A prioritized backlog
3. An implementation recommendation

The engagement is complete when you, as sponsor, confirm that we have delivered all three.

## Fee and payment

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Effort | 80 hours |
| Rate | $200 per hour |
| Total | $16,000 |
| First payment | $8,000 (50%) at kickoff |
| Second payment | $8,000 (50%) on delivery |

## What the fee excludes

This engagement covers discovery only. We will not perform implementation, integrations or production support under this proposal, and we do not guarantee savings. The implementation recommendation will describe possible follow-on work, which would need a separate agreement.

## What we need from you

Your team supplies interview access and source documents. The process map and backlog depend on both, so please tell us before kickoff if any interviewee or document will be hard to obtain.

## How to proceed

1. Sign this proposal.
2. Name a client contact for the engagement.
3. We then agree a start date with that contact. We cannot set a date until we have both the signature and the named contact.
4. We invoice the first $8,000 at kickoff and begin the four weeks of discovery.
5. We deliver the three artifacts, you confirm delivery, and we invoice the remaining $8,000.
