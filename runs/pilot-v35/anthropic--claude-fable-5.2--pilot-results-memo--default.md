# Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Decision

## Recommendation

**Do not scale now.** The pilot missed the required quality threshold, and the speed result is not yet reliable enough to support a scaling decision.

## Results

| Measure | Baseline | Pilot | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tickets | 200 | 100 | Smaller pilot sample |
| Mean handling time | 15 min | 12 min | 3 minutes faster (20%) |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% | Below 92% threshold |
| Cash savings | n/a | None measured | Unproven |

## What the results support

- **Handling time was lower in the pilot.** Mean handling time was 12 minutes against 15 at baseline.
- **Quality fell below the scaling requirement.** The pass rate was 89%, 3 points short of the 92% minimum and 5 points below baseline.

## What the results do not support

- **That the pilot caused the time reduction.** Ticket mix differed between groups and allocation was not randomized, so the difference may reflect the tickets handled, not the pilot itself.
- **That the pilot caused the quality drop.** The same limitation applies; the decline may be real or an artifact of ticket mix.
- **Any financial benefit.** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

## Next decision

**Owner: COO.** Decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up.

We recommend authorizing it. Comparing like-for-like tickets would show whether the handling time gain holds and whether quality meets the 92% threshold. Scaling should be reconsidered only after those results are in.
