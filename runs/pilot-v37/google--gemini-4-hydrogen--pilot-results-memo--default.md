**Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Recommendation**

**Recommendation**
Do not scale the pilot now. 

**What the Results Support**
* **Quality:** The pilot achieved an 89% quality pass rate, down from 94% at baseline. This is below the required 92% threshold for scaling.
* **Handling Time:** Mean handling time was 12 minutes in the pilot (100 tickets) versus 15 minutes at baseline (200 tickets). However, because the ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, these results do not confirm that the pilot caused the reduction.
* **Costs:** No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

Taken together, the current results do not support scaling the pilot.
