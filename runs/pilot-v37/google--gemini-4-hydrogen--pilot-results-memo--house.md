Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Recommendation

Recommendation: Do not scale the pilot now.

What the results support
The pilot does not meet the quality requirement for scaling. The quality pass rate was 89% in the pilot, compared with 94% at baseline, which is below the 92% minimum threshold. Mean handling time fell from 15 minutes across 200 baseline tickets to 12 minutes across 100 pilot tickets, but ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized. Because of those differences, the data cannot confirm whether the pilot caused the faster handling time. No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.
