# Discovery Engagement Proposal

**Scope & Deliverables**
This engagement covers four weeks of discovery only. We will deliver three artifacts:
* Current-state process map
* Prioritized backlog
* Implementation recommendation

**Exclusions**
Implementation, integrations, production support, and guaranteed savings are excluded from this engagement.

**Commercial Offer**
* **Effort & Rate:** 80 hours at $200/hour
* **Total Fee:** $16,000
* **Payment Terms:** 50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery

**Client Responsibilities & Acceptance**
* **Client Inputs:** The client will supply interview access and source documents.
* **Acceptance:** The client sponsor will confirm delivery of the three listed artifacts.

**How to Proceed**
To approve this engagement, please sign this proposal and provide a named client contact. The start date will be agreed upon only after signature and receipt of the named client contact.
