**Executive Readout: Pilot Scale Decision**

**Results Summary**
- Baseline: 200 tickets, 15 min mean handling time; 94% quality pass rate.  
- Pilot: 100 tickets, 12 min mean handling time; 89% quality pass rate.  
- Handling time improved 17%, but ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, limiting causal inference.  
- Quality fell 5 percentage points below the 92% required scaling threshold.  
- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured.

**Decision Support**
The pilot does not currently support scaling. The quality shortfall is the primary blocker, and the non‑randomized design confounds the handling time gain. Cost impacts remain unknown.

**Next Decision**
Sam will run a matched‑ticket follow‑up to isolate quality effects. The COO must authorize this follow‑up before any re‑evaluation of scale can proceed.
