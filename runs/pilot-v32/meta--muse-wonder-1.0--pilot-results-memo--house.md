# Executive readout: pilot scaling decision

## Recommendation

Do not scale the pilot now.

## Results

| Measure | Baseline | Pilot |
|---|---|---|
| Tickets | 200 | 100 |
| Mean handling time | 15 minutes | 12 minutes |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% |

The pilot mean was 3 minutes lower than the baseline mean. Ticket mix differed between the groups, and allocation was not randomized, so the results do not show that the pilot caused the lower handling time.

The pilot quality pass rate of 89% is below the required scaling threshold of at least 92%. The baseline rate was 94%.

No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured, so the results do not support a savings claim.

## Next decision

The COO decides whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up. Scaling should wait for that decision and the follow-up results.
