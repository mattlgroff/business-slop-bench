Discovery Engagement Proposal  
Client Sponsor

This document proposes a four-week discovery engagement to produce a current-state process map, a prioritized backlog, and an implementation recommendation. The engagement requires 80 hours of work at $200/hour, totaling $16,000. Payment is 50% at kickoff and 50% upon delivery.

The client supplies interview access and source documents. The start date will be agreed after signature and a named client contact.

Excluded from scope are implementation, integrations, production support and guaranteed savings. Acceptance requires the sponsor's confirmation of delivery of the three artifacts: the process map, the backlog, and the recommendation.
