Executive Readout: Pilot Scaling Decision

**Recommendation: Do Not Scale Now**

| Metric | Baseline | Pilot |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tickets | 200 | 100 |
| Mean handling time | 15 minutes | 12 minutes |
| Quality pass rate | 94% | 89% |

**What the results support**

- Mean handling time was observed to be 3 minutes lower in the pilot. However, ticket mix differed and allocation was not randomized, so the results do not support attributing this difference to the pilot.
- Pilot quality was 89%, below the required scaling threshold of at least 92% and below the 94% baseline. The scaling condition is not met.
- No staffing reductions or cash savings have been measured, so no cost saving is supported.

**Next decision**

The COO is to decide whether to authorize Sam to run a matched-ticket follow-up before any scaling decision is revisited.
